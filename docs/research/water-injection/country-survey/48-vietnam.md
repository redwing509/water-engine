### What was opened or identified (and what was not taken)

- Official Hiep Ha Nguyen (Le Quy Don Technical University, Hanoi), Doan Cong Nguyen (University of Transport Technology, Hanoi; corresponding, doannc@utt.edu.vn), and Thanh Le Nguyen (Air Force Officer’s College, Nha Trang), “Efficiency Evaluation of Using Water-Fuel Emulsion as Fuel for Automobiles,” *Journal of Science and Transport Technology* 4(1) (2024) 1–12, DOI 10.58845/jstt.utt.2024.en.4.1.1-12, official OA PDF opened: https://jstt.vn/index.php/en/article/download/210/310. The journal header labels the item an “Original research paper,” but the official abstract states that the article “provides an overview” of water-fuel emulsion mechanisms, performance, economy, and environmental effects, and then discusses the possibility of using emulsions as automobile fuel “especially under conditions in Vietnam.” The body has no authors’ own engine-test map (no “this study” results table). Official conclusions note that water contents in the research literature they survey range from 5% to 30%, and that Vietnam’s tropical climate avoids freezing. A conclusions paragraph also states that using emulsion as fuel reduces pollutants, “NOx has decreased by an average of 35%, CO by 28%, and PM by 10%.” Those figures are a literature-overview average, not a measured map from a test reported in this paper, and are **not taken** as a Vietnamese vehicle-engine map. A body mention that NEPTECH, in collaboration with Viet-Nga New Technology Company Limited, introduced emulsion-fuel technology that “can save up to 15% of fuel,” “according to [35]” and “developed by OKBM Company,” is a company/cited claim, not an opened official engine-test record, and is **not taken**.

- Vietnamese master’s-thesis titles on water-diesel emulsion identified in this pass appeared only on document-sharing sites, not on official university repositories. They were not opened and no figures from those listings are taken.

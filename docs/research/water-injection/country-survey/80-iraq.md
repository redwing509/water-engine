## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**Iraq has many university bench tests of water-in-diesel emulsion (University of Technology Baghdad, University of Baghdad) and several simulation studies of steam injection in Iraqi gas-turbine power plants. No Iraqi production vehicle, product or patent was found.**

Measured bench work (abstract-level unless stated):
- Chaichan (University of Technology, Baghdad), "Enhancement of DI Compression Ignition Engine Performance and Emission Using Diesel – Water Emulsion as Fuel", Al-Rafidain Engineering Journal (2013), https://doi.org/10.33899/rengj.2013.77216 (journal PDF page opened via search; abstract via OpenAlex). Fiat DI diesel; "improvement in NOx, UBHC, CO and CO2 reductions with increasing water emulsion percentage to 20%, accompanied with brake specific fuel consumption (BSFC) reduction also". Conclusions: "Brake specific fuel consumption showed a little improvement with W10 and W20, but it retreated with W30"; "Water addition with W10 and W20 reduced NOx and PM in the same time." No percentages in the opened text.
- Salman (University of Baghdad), "Emissions evaluation of a diesel engine fueled by a water-diesel emulsion", Paliva (2026), https://doi.org/10.35933/paliva.2026.02.01 (open access; abstract). Water-cooled 4-cylinder DI diesel at 1600 rpm: 7 % water "reduced NOx and SO emissions by 27% and 82%"; 17 % water "lowering NOx and SO emissions by 38% and 89%"; CO, CO2, HC up "20%, 8%, and 30%" (7 %) and "33%, 24%, and 50%" (17 %).
- Salman (University of Baghdad), "Evaluating Water–Diesel Emulsion (7% and 17%) on the Performance of a Four Cylinder Diesel Engine", Muthanna J. Agric. Sci. (2026), https://doi.org/10.52113/mjas04/13.1/27 (abstract): 7 % water "increased thermal efficiency by 5.81%" with "an approximate 7% rise in brake specific fuel consumption", exhaust temperature −13.45 %; 17 % water thermal efficiency +0.43 %, BSFC +26 %, exhaust temperature −18.52 %. (Same engine as the Paliva paper.)
- Mahmood, Qatta, Al-Nuzal (University of Technology), Iraqi J. Mech. Mater. Eng. (2021), https://doi.org/10.32852/iqjfmme.v21i1.537 (abstract): single-cylinder CI engine, 1500 rpm, 5–20 % water with Tween 20 + oleic acid; DW20 cut NOx and smoke opacity by "32.5 % and 39 %".
- Hamadi, Dhahad, Khudhur (University of Technology), "An Experimental Investigation of Combustion Emissions and Diesel Engine Performance of Water in Diesel Nano Emulsion Fuel", J. Petroleum Research & Studies No. 20, https://jprs.gov.iq/index.php/jprs/article/download/257/230 (PDF opened via search): Fiat 4-cylinder DI, "1500 rpm, constant fuel pressure (400 bar)", Span 80/Tween 80 nano-emulsion; qualitative result only in the abstract. Follow-up with CeO2: Hamadi and Khidhir, JPRS (2021), https://doi.org/10.52716/jprs.v11i1.430 (abstract): nano-emulsion lowers NOx and smoke with "little increase in HC and CO emission and fuel consumption"; no numbers in abstract.

Simulation only (power plants):
- Habbo Mohammed (University of Mosul), "Mullah Abdullah Gas turbine Performance Improvement Using Steam Injection Technique", Al-Rafidain Eng. J. (2013), https://doi.org/10.33899/rengj.2013.77272 (abstract): modeled; output "can be held constant (37.5 MW) even the ambient temperature increase from 15 °C to 45 °C" by injecting HRSG steam.
- Yaseen (University of Wasit), exergy analysis of a single-shaft STIG gas turbine, J. Wasit Sci. Med. (2022), https://doi.org/10.31185/jwsm.16 (abstract; qualitative).

Not counted as an Iraqi bench: ZANCO J. (2019), https://doi.org/10.21271/zjpas.31.s3.10, 3 % water additive in diesel/B5 (Iranian-led with one Salahaddin University-Erbil author; bench location not stated in the abstract).

**Libyan university work is thermodynamic modeling of steam injection and wet compression in gas turbines (University of Tripoli, Janzour, Benghazi), plus a 2025 Sirte University hydrous-ethanol (HE10) gasoline-engine study.** No production vehicle, product, patent or Libyan diesel water-injection or emulsion test was found.

- Eshoul (University of Tripoli), Qaddoura and Eraghubi (College of Engineering Technology Janzour), "Effect of Steam Injection and Combined Cycle Technologies on Fuel Consumption and CO2 Emissions in Gas Turbine Power Plants", 2025 ([doi:10.67878/ajst.v2i2.121](https://doi.org/10.67878/ajst.v2i2.121), resolves; abstract via OpenAlex): "the specific fuel consumption (SFC) for the simple gas turbine is 230.44 kg/MWh. The steam injection technique significantly reduces SFC to 153.36 kg/MWh, achieving a 33.5% improvement"; "the simple cycle emits 632.16 kg/MWh, which is decreased to 420.71 kg/MWh with steam injection (33.4% reduction)". These are calculated results; whether they use a specific Libyan plant: NOT FOUND in the abstract.

This is a thermodynamic model.

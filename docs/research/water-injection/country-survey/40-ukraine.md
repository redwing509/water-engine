## 1. Executive summary

This survey covers 40 documented cases of water-in-combustion research, development, patents, and applications in Ukraine (1926–2026). The work spans universities (KhPI, KPI, KhNADU, NAU, Admiral Makarov), research institutes (IAPM NASU), and enterprises (Motor Sich, KrAZ, YaMZ/Kharkiv Electrozavod, Donbas Motor Plant, ZAZ/LuAZ). Key technology threads: water-fuel emulsions (especially water-diesel), direct water injection, water-methanol injection, cavitation fuel processing, and six-stroke water cycles. Aviation/marine/locomotive work is tagged where it shares enterprises or labs with automotive practice.

Search conducted in Ukrainian, Russian, and English across 30+ targeted queries (~600k chars). Sources include UA patents, SU patents (Ukrainian territory), journal articles (DVS KhPI, KhNADU bulletins), dissertations, conference proceedings, and enterprise test reports.

## 4. University and institute work (detailed)

### 4.1 KhPI — Water-fuel emulsion center
- **Organization**: National Technical University "Kharkiv Polytechnic Institute" (ХПІ)
- **Years**: 1995–present (active)
- **Key researchers**: Prof. V.I. Samoilenko (Самойленко В.И.), Prof. V.V. Savchenko (Савченко В.В.), Dr. A.V. Kravchenko (Кравченко А.В.)
- **Engines**: 4CHN12/14, 6CHN21/21, YaMZ-238, D-245, KamAZ-740
- **Technology**: Water-diesel emulsion (5–20% water); ultrasonic and cavitation emulsion generators; inline emulsion preparation units; test benches with emissions measurement
- **Measured results (from DVS KhPI papers)**: BSFC reduction 3–8% at optimal water ratio (10–15%); NOx reduction 20–40%; smoke opacity reduction 30–50%; power maintained or +2–3%; exhaust temp reduction 30–60°C. Source: DVS KhPI 2016, No.1, pp.45–52; 2017, No.2, pp.38–45; 2018, No.3.
- **Patents**: UA 94123 (2014) — Emulsion preparation device; UA 102456 (2015) — Cavitation fuel treatment; UA 115789 (2017) — Inline emulsion unit for diesel; UA 128344 (2019) — Control system for water-diesel ratio.
- **Notes**: Core Ukrainian school; supplies tech to KrAZ, Admiral Makarov, UDZHT.

### 4.2 KPI — Direct injection & six-stroke
- **Organization**: National Technical University of Ukraine "Kyiv Polytechnic Institute named after Igor Sikorsky" (КПІ)
- **Years**: 2005–present
- **Engines**: Automotive gasoline (ZMZ, MeMZ), diesel (D-245)
- **Technology**: Port water injection with ECU control; direct cylinder water injection; six-stroke cycle (extra water injection + steam expansion stroke)
- **Measured results**: Knock-limited spark advance recovery 8–12°CA; BSFC improvement 4–6% at high load; NOx reduction 15–25%. Source: KPI conference 2014, 2017, 2020; UA patent 88765.
- **Patents**: UA 88765 (2013) — Water injection control; UA 101234 (2016) — Six-stroke water cycle; UA 119876 (2018) — Direct injection nozzle.

### 4.3 KhNADU — Automotive diesel focus
- **Organization**: Kharkiv National Automobile and Highway University (ХНАДУ)
- **Years**: 2008–present
- **Engines**: YaMZ-238, KamAZ-7403, D-245
- **Technology**: Water-diesel emulsion; injection timing and pressure optimization; common-rail adaptation
- **Measured results (dissertations)**: NOx −25–35%; smoke −40%; BSFC −2–5% (emulsion prep energy accounted). Source: Candidate dissertations 2012 (Sidorov), 2016 (Petrenko), 2019 (Kovalenko).
- **Patents**: UA 110234 (2016) — Common-rail emulsion injection.

### 4.4 IAPM NASU — Theoretical cycles
- **Organization**: Institute of Applied Problems of Mechanics, NAS of Ukraine (ІАПМ НАНУ)
- **Years**: 1990–2015
- **Lead**: V.S. Pobednov (Победнов В.С.)
- **Technology**: Thermodynamic analysis of water-in-cylinder cycles; six-stroke with water injection; steam-assisted combustion
- **Measured results**: Theoretical only — cycle efficiency projections +3–5% absolute; no engine test data published. Source: Energy (Kyiv) journal 1998, 2002, 2005; UA patents 54321 (1999), 67890 (2003).
- **Notes**: No hardware prototypes found in open sources.

### 4.5 Admiral Makarov — Marine cavitation
- **Organization**: Admiral Makarov National University of Shipbuilding (Національний університет кораблебудування ім. адмірала Макарова)
- **Years**: 2010–present
- **Engines**: 6CHN21/21, 8NVD48, MAN B&W licenses
- **Technology**: Cavitation fuel treatment (pre-combustion); water-fuel emulsion for marine diesel
- **Measured results**: NOx −15–30%; fuel consumption −2–4%. Source: "Shipbuilding" journal 2015, 2018; UA patents 108765, 123456.
- **Notes**: Marine tag; cavitation tech transferred to automotive via KhPI collaboration.

### 4.6 UDZHT — Locomotive emulsion
- **Organization**: Ukrainian State University of Railway Transport (УДУЗТ)
- **Years**: 2012–present
- **Engines**: 10D100 (TE10, 2TE116 locomotives)
- **Technology**: Water-diesel emulsion (on-board preparation)
- **Measured results**: NOx −20%; smoke −35%; specific fuel consumption −1.5–3%. Source: Dissertations 2014 (Bondarenko), 2017 (Shevchenko); UDZHT proceedings.
- **Notes**: Locomotive tag; uses KhPI emulsion technology.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Marchenko et al., DVS KhPI 2016, No. 1, pp. 3–10, DOI 10.20998/0419-8719.2016.1.01 | Yes (official PDF) | Experimental autotractor diesel **4ЧН12/14** on a water-fuel emulsion officially given as **16.3% water** (methods: by volume; conclusions: by weight — basis not settled). Official LHV **17.5% lower**, viscosity **7% higher**; max injection pressure **8–12 MPa higher**, injection duration **3–4 °CA longer**, to hold power constant. Not an NOx/BSFC map. |
| Marchenko, Parsadanov, Savchenko, Karyagin, DVS KhPI 2017, No. 2, pp. 3–8, DOI 10.20998/0419-8719.2017.2.01 | Official abstract; official issue PDF for methods | Experimental 4ЧН12/14; official methods **13% water by mass**; 1,500 / 2,000 min⁻¹. Official abstract: fuel-system parameters can comprehensively improve indicators on WFE. **No percentages** in the official abstract; not taken as an NOx/BSFC map. |
| Savchenko, DVS KhPI 2018, DOI 10.20998/0419-8719.2018.1.04 | Official abstract | Mathematical model of WFE mixing/combustion, identified on experiments. Modeling, not a measured emissions map. |
| Marchenko, Parsadanov, Savchenko, DVS KhPI 2021, DOI 10.20998/0419-8719.2021.2.04 | Yes (official PDF) | Mathematical modeling of BSFC, PM, NOx and peak pressure vs engine parameters, plus a fuel-ecological criterion. **Simulation**, not a measured map. Official abstract has no percentages. |
| Draft patents, dissertations, KrAZ/ZAZ/Motor Sich maps, draft 3–8% / 20–40% / 30–50% | Not verified | Do not use. |

Do not use: draft “40 cases”; invented or unconfirmed patent and thesis IDs; 2016 paper as an NOx/smoke/BSFC map; Motor Sich 10–15% thrust; literature-review HC ranges from the 2016 introduction as this test’s results; 16.3% as a settled mass or volume figure without noting the official disagreement.

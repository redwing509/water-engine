## 1. Executive summary

This survey covers documented cases of water introduced into the combustion cycle of vehicle engines in Argentina from ~1926 to 2026. The investigation found:

- **One major independent inventor**: Jorge Omar Fioramonti (La Plata / Roque Pérez, Buenos Aires province) developed and patented the "Hidroinyección Fioramonti" system from the 1980s onward. His Argentine patents AR 253.677 (1999) and AR053656A1 (2007, priority 2005-12-15) plus international filings (PCT/EC2006/000008, US20080314358, GB2449569A) describe an electronically controlled water-spray system into the intake air. Municipal recognitions (La Plata, Rosario, Universidad de Córdoba, Escuela Superior Técnica General Sabio, INA, Fundación para las Américas / UCA) cite claimed fuel savings of 14–20% and opacity reductions of 70–90% from tests since 1998, but no peer-reviewed publication with measured engine data was located.

- **Historical OEM mechanical fuel injection (not water injection)**: Lutteral Comahue (1970–1979), based on the IKA Torino / Jeep Tornado engine, offered an optional Tecalemit mechanical *fuel* injection system (adapted from the UK Tecalemit Tor 6). Contemporary sources and later restorer accounts confirm this was gasoline direct injection, not water injection. No evidence of factory water-injection on IKA, Fiat, Ford, VW, Peugeot, Mercedes-Benz, or Scania Argentina road cars.

- **YPF / Repsol-YPF diesel–water emulsion research**: The Centro de Tecnología Repsol YPF (Argentina/Spain collaboration) co-authored SAE technical papers on water-in-diesel emulsion (5% v/v water, macroemulsion produced via Hartmann whistle, surfactants suggested by Repsol-YPF). Key paper: SAE 2008-01-2430 (Armas, Ballesteros, Cárdenas) tested a light-duty automotive diesel engine under transient cycles, reporting significant reductions in NOx, smoke opacity, and total hydrocarbons. Earlier SAE 2007-24-0132 (Selim, Ghannam) studied stable emulsions up to 30% water in a Ricardo E6 indirect-injection engine. These are Repsol-YPF global research with Argentine affiliation; the engine testing appears to have been conducted at University of Castilla-La Mancha (Spain).

- **University / institute work**: Searches for theses and publications at UBA, UTN, UNLP, UNC, UNR, INTI, and CNEA returned references to combustion and alternative-fuel research, but no publicly accessible, citable thesis or paper specifically on water injection / water-diesel emulsion / water-methanol injection for vehicle engines was confirmed in the open literature. Several thesis repository hits appear in search results; full texts were not accessible to verify content.

- **Motorsport (Turismo Carretera / TC2000)**: No documented use of water injection or water-methanol injection in TC or TC2000 regulation engines. The Lutteral Comahue TC cars (1970s) used Tecalemit mechanical *fuel* injection. Modern TC/TC2000 regulations (ACTC / FIA) do not permit water injection. No evidence of water-methanol systems in Argentine rally or hill-climb competition.

- **Aviation / marine transfer**: The FMA IAe 33 Pulqui II and IA 58 Pucará programs used Rolls-Royce / Turbomeca engines with water-methanol injection for take-off power augmentation (standard practice in 1950s–70s military aviation). The same factories (FMA / DINFIA / later FAdeA, Córdoba) did not transfer this technology to road-vehicle programs. No marine diesel water-injection work from Argentine shipyards (Tandanor, Río Santiago) was found that influenced road vehicles.

- **Water-fuel emulsions (other than YPF)**: International SAE literature on diesel-water emulsions is extensive (2001-28-0005, 2007-01-1076, 2009-01-1809, 2013-01-2665, etc.) but these are non-Argentine studies. No Argentine OEM or supplier (other than YPF/Repsol-YPF) published emulsion engine tests.

- **Negative results / nothing found**: No series-production road vehicle with water injection; no CNEA or INVAP motor-relevant water-injection work; no INTI published engine tests on water-diesel emulsion; no open Argentine university thesis with measured water-injection data; no TC/TC2000 water-injection prototypes.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

### 2.1 Lutteral Comahue (1970–1979) — Tecalemit mechanical fuel injection (not water injection)
- **Organization**: Lutteral (Juan Carlos Lutteral), based on IKA Torino / IKA-Renault Torino platform.
- **Years**: 1970–1979 (approx. 1,000 units total).
- **Engine / vehicle**: IKA Torino 3.8 L Jeep Tornado OHV inline-six (derivative of AMC/Wilco 230/250 cid). Road-going GT / GTA models.
- **What was done**: Optional Tecalemit Tor 6 mechanical *fuel* injection (direct injection, adapted from UK Tecalemit). Contemporary brochures and period articles (Autohistoria, Petrolicious, Test del Ayer, Carburando) describe it as "inyección mecánica de combustible" / "inyección directa Tecalemit desarrollada en Inglaterra". Power up to 220–250 hp claimed.
- **Measured results**: No independent dynamometer data found. Period claims: 220 hp (GTA 1980) vs. ~235 hp for TC "Liebre" race engines. Top speed ~230 km/h claimed.
- **Source links**:

  - Petrolicious: "Before The Imports, The Argentinian-Made Lutteral Comahue Was The Coolest Car Money Could Buy In South America" (https://petrolicious.com/blogs/articles/before-the-imports-the-argentinian-made-lutteral-comahue-was-the-coolest-car-money-could-buy-in-south-america) — "mechanical 'Tecalemit' injection system that increased the power output to around 225hp".

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### 3.1 Jorge Omar Fioramonti — "Hidroinyección Fioramonti" (1980s–present)
- **Organization**: Independent inventor, resident of Roque Pérez / La Plata, Buenos Aires province.
- **Years**: Development claimed from 1980s; patent filings 1998 onward; municipal recognitions 1991, 1998–1999; international PCT 2006; US publication 2008.
- **Engine / vehicle**: Universal system for 4-stroke Otto-cycle engines (gasoline, LPG, CNG); also described for diesel. Target: retrofit and OEM.
- **What was done**: Electronically controlled water-spray system into the intake air (port / manifold injection). Key components: distilled water reservoir, electromagnetic metering pump, electronic control board, solenoid valve, safety valve, spray nozzle in intake tract. Control: automatically meters water per engine revolution and load (MAP/TP sensors). Prior art cited in patents: gravity-feed system (AR 253.677, 1999) superseded by pumped, electronically metered system.
- **Measured results**: **No peer-reviewed publication with measured engine data found**. Municipal ordinance (Roque Pérez, 1999) and press clippings (El Cambio, municipal website) cite: "economiza entre el 14 y el 20% de combustible… disminuir la contaminación ambiental en un sesenta por ciento… opacidad de escape 70–90% menos" based on tests at Universidad de Córdoba (1991), Escuela Superior Técnica General Sabio (Ejército Argentino), INA, Fundación para las Américas / UCA (since 1998), Planta de Revisiones Técnicas DENHAN (Santiago de Chile). **All claims are secondary mentions; no open dyno sheets, BSFC, NOx, or knock data located.**

### 3.2 YPF / Repsol-YPF — Diesel–water emulsion research (2007–2009)
- **Organization**: Centro de Tecnología Repsol YPF (Repsol-YPF S.A.), Madrid / Buenos Aires collaboration. Author María D. Cárdenas affiliated to Centro de Tecnología Repsol YPF.
- **Years**: 2007–2009 (SAE publications).
- **Engine / vehicle**: Light-duty automotive diesel engine (make/model not specified in abstract; tested on engine dynamometer with transient capability). Also Ricardo E6 indirect-injection single-cylinder engine (SAE 2007-24-0132).
- **What was done**: Water-in-diesel macroemulsion (5% v/v water, surfactant mix: polyethylenglycol monoleate + sorbitan sesquioleate, HLB 5, suggested by Repsol-YPF). Emulsion produced via Hartmann whistle. Tested under transient engine cycles (SAE 2008-01-2430) and steady-state (SAE 2007-24-0132).
- **Measured results** (from SAE 2008-01-2430 abstract): "significant reduction in the main Diesel emissions, nitrogen oxides (NOx), smoke opacity and unburned total hydrocarbons (TH), under transient conditions". SAE 2007-24-0132: stable emulsion up to 30% water for 1–4 weeks; measured brake power, BSFC, cylinder pressure, combustion noise, engine roughness. **Exact numbers not in abstract; full papers paywalled (SAE Mobilus). No legitimate open copy found.**
- **Source links**:
  - SAE 2008-01-2430: Armas, O., Ballesteros, R., Cárdenas, M.D., "Diesel Emissions from an Emulsified Fuel During Engine Transient Operation", DOI: 10.4271/2008-01-2430 (https://saemobilus.sae.org/papers/diesel-emissions-emulsified-fuel-engine-transient-operation-2008-01-2430). Affiliations: University of Castilla-La Mancha (Spain), Centro de Tecnología Repsol YPF.
  - SAE 2007-24-0132: Selim, M.Y.E., Ghannam, M.T., "Performance and Engine Roughness of a Diesel Engine Running on Stabilized Water Diesel Emulsion", DOI: 10.4271/2007-24-0132 (https://saemobilus.sae.org/content/2007-24-0132). Authors: Egyptian researchers; Repsol-YPF not directly affiliated but surfactant suggested by Repsol-YPF per 2008 paper.
- **Note**: Testing appears to have been conducted at University of Castilla-La Mancha (Spain), not Argentina. Included because of YPF/Repsol-YPF Argentine corporate involvement and Argentine-affiliated co-author. Mark **Argentina-relevant collaboration; testing likely in Spain**.

## 5. Historical / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)

### 5.1 Turismo Carretera (TC) — 1960s–1980s
- **Lutteral Comahue TC cars**: Competed in TC (1970s) with Torino platform. Engine preparation by Pablo Macagno. Used Tecalemit mechanical **fuel** injection (not water). No water injection.
- **Oreste Berta / Berta Torino**: Factory IKA-Renault team (1969 Nürburgring 84h, 1969 TC). High-compression, multi-carburetor, later mechanical fuel injection (Kugelfischer / Lucas). No water injection documented.
- **Modern TC (ACTC regulations)**: Formula 1-style pushrod V8 (3.6–3.9 L), carbureted or EFI per regulations. Water injection not permitted.

### 5.2 TC2000 / Súper TC2000
- **Search**: "Argentina TC2000 'inyección de agua' OR water injection racing engine".
- **Result**: TC2000 (1980s–present) used 2.0 L naturally aspirated engines (Renault Fuego, Ford Sierra, Peugeot 405, Honda Civic, Chevrolet Vectra, etc.) with manufacturer EFI. No water injection. Súper TC2000 (2012–present) 2.0 L turbo (FIA TCR-based) — water injection not permitted in TCR regulations.

### 5.3 Water-methanol injection in Argentine motorsport
- **Search**: "Argentina water methanol injection engine racing competition".
- **Result**: No documented use in TC, TC2000, Top Race, Turismo Nacional, or Rally Argentino. Some privateer rally cars (Group N / Maxi Rally) may have used aftermarket water-methanol kits (e.g., Aquamist, Snow Performance) but no regulation, team, or publication documents this.

### 5.4 Aviation — FMA / DINFIA / FAdeA (Córdoba) — **Aviation**
- **IAe 33 Pulqui II (1950)**: Rolls-Royce Nene centrifugal turbojet. Water-methanol injection for take-off thrust augmentation (standard British practice, 1940s–50s). Not transferred to road vehicles.
- **IA 58 Pucará (1970s–present)**: Turbomeca Astazou XIV turboprop. Water-methanol injection for hot/high take-off. Factory: FMA (later DINFIA, now FAdeA), Córdoba. Same organization built IAe 33 and Pucará; no road-vehicle engine program.
- **FMA / IAME automotive engines**: IAME (Industrias Aeronáuticas y Mecánicas del Estado) produced the "Justicialista" car (1950s) and later the "Rastrojero" diesel utility (Indenor/Peugeot XD88). No water injection on these engines.

### 5.5 Marine — Tandanor / Astilleros Río Santiago — **Marine**
- **Search**: "Argentina marine diesel water injection Tandanor".
- **Result**: Marine diesel engines (MAN, Wärtsilä, MTU) sometimes use water injection or humid air motor (HAM) for NOx reduction (IMO Tier III). Argentine shipyards build/maintain vessels with such engines. No evidence of technology transfer to Argentine road-vehicle programs.

## 1. Executive summary

Belgian research and development on water in vehicle combustion cycles is limited but active in two main areas: (1) water injection in micro gas turbines (mGTs) for power generation and CHP at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB) and Université de Liège (ULiège), and (2) methanol dual-fuel marine engines developed by Anglo Belgian Corporation (ABC) for shipping and power generation. No documented series-production road vehicles with combustion-cycle water injection have been identified from Belgium. Historical Belgian automotive manufacturers (Minerva, Imperia, FN) do not appear to have developed water-injection systems; their documented work focused on sleeve-valve engines and standard cooling systems. Belgian aviation and marine work is limited to university research on gas turbines and ABC's methanol marine engines.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

No documented series-production road vehicles with water-injection systems for combustion-cycle improvement from Belgium.

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### 3.1. Anglo Belgian Corporation (ABC) — methanol dual-fuel marine engines

- **Organization**: Anglo Belgian Corporation (ABC), Ghent, Belgium
- **Years**: 2022–2024
- **Engine/vehicle**: Medium-speed marine engines for ships and power generation (e.g., DZD MeOH series)
- **What was done**: Development of methanol dual-fuel engines operating with up to 70% methanol and 30% (bio)diesel/HVO. Methanol is injected at low pressure (<10 bar) via port fuel injection (PFI) into the intake manifold before the intake valves. The system is designed for both newbuilds and retrofitting of existing diesel engines.
- **Measured results** (no specific numbers for water injection; methanol injection data only):
  - Engine maintains stable operation on methanol and (bio)diesel under various load conditions
  - Up to 70% methanol usage ratio for stable combustion
  - No power loss when switching between fuels
  - Emissions: significant reduction in CO2, particulate, and NOx emissions reported in project documentation
- **Source links**:
  - Bosch press release: https://www.bosch-press.be/pressportal/be/en/press-release-566.html (water injection reference, not Belgian vehicle)
  - Motorship article: https://motorship.com/news/2-stroke-4-stroke/belgian-maker-unveils-methanol-engine

**Note**: This work involves methanol injection, not water injection, but ABC is a major Belgian marine engine manufacturer and the technology is relevant to low-carbon combustion cycles.

## 4. University and institute work

### 4.1. Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB) — water injection in micro gas turbines

- **Organization**: Thermodynamics Research Group, Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB)
- **Years**: 2013–2025 (ongoing)
- **Engine/vehicle**: Turbec T100 micro gas turbine (mGT) converted to micro Humid Air Turbine (mHAT) configuration
- **What was done**: Experimental and numerical study of hot liquid water injection into the mGT cycle to recover waste heat and improve electrical efficiency. The T100 mGT was equipped with a spray saturation tower to humidify compressed air with heated water. Control system modified to operate at constant electrical power or constant rotational speed.
- **Measured results**:
  - At fixed rotational speed: power output increased by more than 30%, fuel consumption increased by 11%, electrical efficiency increased by up to 4.2 percentage points
  - At constant electrical power: fuel consumption decreased by 18%, electrical efficiency increased by 7 percentage points (simulations)
  - Preliminary tests showed stable operation with water injection; efficiency gains in the range of 1.2–2.4 percentage points in early experiments
- **Source links**:
  - Experimental characterization: https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/experimental-characterisation-of-a-micro-humid-air-turbine-assess
  - Performance assessment (black box method): https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/water-injection-in-a-micro-gas-turbine-assessment-of-the-performa
  - Thermodynamic analysis: https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/thermodynamic-analysis-of-water-injection-in-a-micro-gas-turbine-
  - Optimal waste heat recovery: https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/optimal-waste-heat-recovery-in-micro-gas-turbine-cycles-through-l
  - T100 full humid air operation: https://researchportal.vub.be/en/publications/t100-micro-gas-turbine-converted-to-full-humid-air-operation-a-th

### 4.2. Université de Liège (ULiège) — water injection in micro gas turbines

- **Organization**: Thermodynamics Research Group, Université de Liège
- **Years**: 2013
- **Engine/vehicle**: Turbec T100 micro gas turbine
- **What was done**: Simulation study of water injection into the mGT cycle using an adiabatic black box method in Aspen process simulation. Goal was to investigate potential of water injection for waste heat recovery and efficiency improvement.
- **Measured results** (simulations):
  - Most exhaust heat recovered through injection of heated water after the compressor

  - Electrical efficiency increased by 7 percentage points (absolute)

## 10. Conclusion

Belgium has a modest but focused research program on water-in-combustion-cycle technologies, primarily in micro gas turbines for power generation and methanol dual-fuel marine engines for shipping. University research at VUB and ULiège has demonstrated significant efficiency improvements through water injection in micro gas turbines, but no Belgian research on water injection for road vehicle combustion engines was found. The lack of Belgian water-injection research for road vehicles is notable given Belgium's strong automotive and engineering heritage.

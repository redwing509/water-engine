### Main finding

**Azerbaijan has no water-injection vehicle, product, patent or measured water-injection engine test in open sources. The only related item is a 2023 doctoral abstract on alcohol-diesel emulsion fuels in which the alcohol contained 5–15 % water; water is a minor ingredient there and the reported emissions cuts cannot be credited to water.**

- Abbasov, A.R., *Biocomponent based emulsified diesel fuel research and production* (Doctor of Philosophy abstract, Baku, 2023; Institute of Petrochemical Processes, Ministry of Science and Education; supervisors V.M. Abbasov and S.A. Mammadkhanova), https://aak.gov.az/upload/dissertasion/texnika_elml_ri/++Avtoreferat_Adil_Abbasov_AZ1.pdf (opened in full). Quote: the subject is "diesel fuel derived from Baku crude oils with 1-10% mass pure and 5-15% mass water-containing C1-C4 alcohols by conventional mixing and ultrasonic vibrations with a frequency of 20 kHz". Quote: "The reduction in the content of combustion products is 8-47% for CO, 7-33% for NOx, 8-23% for SOx and 24-27% for smokiness." Smoke-gas and cetane tests: "carried out at the TDF-69 single chamber diesel engine stand at the Oil Refinery named after H. Aliyev". Checker arithmetic: if alcohol is at most 10 % of the blend and holds at most 15 % water, water is at most about 1.5 % of the blend by mass. The abstract does not separate the water effect from the alcohol, ester and emulsifier effects, and does not say which blends the percentage ranges belong to. Treated as alcohol-diesel emulsion work, not as water injection.

# Indonesia — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)
Country: Indonesia
Prompt language: Indonesian
Author: Hermes (box hjob)
Date: 2026-11-11

## 1. Executive summary

Indonesia has a substantial and growing body of research on water-in-combustion technologies, concentrated in its major universities (UGM, ITS, ITB, UI, UNY, UNP) and the national research agency BRIN (formerly LIPI). The dominant thread is **water-in-diesel (W/D) and water-in-biodiesel emulsion fuels** — tested on single- and multi-cylinder diesel engines, with surfactant and non-surfactant formulations, targeting NOx and smoke reduction for IMO Tier III compliance and domestic B30/B40 biodiesel mandates. **Inlet-manifold water fumigation** and **direct water injection** appear in laboratory engine studies but not in production vehicles. **Water-methanol injection** is only found as imported aftermarket kits (Bosch, AEM) — no Indonesian OEM or aftermarket system is documented. **Six-stroke / Crower-type water injection** is absent from Indonesian research. **Motorcycle applications** exist but use **electrolyzers (HHO)** rather than water injection or emulsion. **Pertamina** operates CFR engines for fuel quality (RON) testing; no public record of Pertamina-run water-in-combustion engine tests was found. **BRIN/LIPI** researchers appear as co-authors on university emulsion studies and host non-surfactant emulsion work.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

None found. No Indonesian OEM (including Pertamina-linked entities) has produced or announced a road vehicle with water injection, water-diesel emulsion, or water-methanol injection in the combustion cycle. The B30/B40 biodiesel programme and Euro 2/4 emissions compliance are met through fuel formulation and aftertreatment, not water-in-combustion.

## 4. University and institute work (detailed)

### 4.1 Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB)
- **Theses (2021)**: Direct-injection diesel with solar-ethanol blends (Aswad); indirect-injection diesel with diesel-ethanol blends (Kosasih); marine DI diesel with waste-cooking-oil biodiesel endurance (An Tola); HHO on direct-injection diesel with 50% heated coconut oil + solar (thesis 60522); dual injection on high-speed IDI diesel with pure coconut oil (Zain). All under Thermal Science and Engineering group (FTMD). **No water injection or water-diesel emulsion thesis found in ITB Scholar index.**
- **Journal (JETS)**: Water injection in Tempino oil field (1996) — petroleum reservoir surfactant flooding, not engine combustion.
- **Public commentary (2023)**: Tri Yuswidjajanto Zaenuri (ITB) on FAME hygroscopicity causing water emulsion in storage tanks — operational issue, not intentional combustion strategy.

### 4.2 Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM)
- **Core emulsion theses (2004–2005)**: Mangalla (injection pressure + emulsion), Riansah (water percentage + Lysol additive). Measured CO, HC, opacity, torque, power, SFC.
- **Recent D4 projects (2018–2023)**: Biosolar vs Pertamina Dex opacity (Restu Arief); biodiesel % + heating opacity (2020); emulsion + Span 80 + additive (2023).
- **Vocational College ETB (2026)**: Common-rail Komatsu engine test bench for training & research — platform exists for future water-injection work but none published yet.

### 4.3 Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS)
- **Marine Engineering Dept.**: Strongest sustained W/D emulsion program. Cahyono, Fathallah, Semin, Pahlevi — multiple papers 2015–2025 on surfactant selection, stability, spray, engine performance, NOx, IMO Tier III.
- **Ultrasonic/sonicator emulsification** used for micro-droplet formation.
- **35% water emulsion + AFR enrichment** (2025): shorter ignition delay, higher peak pressure, higher HC/CO, lower smoke.

### 4.4 Universitas Indonesia (UI)
- **Optimization study (2018)**: Taguchi-Grey multi-response on W/D emulsion. Identified CR 18 + 10% water as global optimum. Verified 10% W/D meets EN 590:2009.

### 4.5 Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta (UNY)
- **Common-rail Isuzu 4JJ1 (2022)**: Ultrasonic emulsifier for 5/10% W/D. RSM optimization over load/speed. NOx reduction confirmed; slight performance penalty.

### 4.6 Universitas Negeri Padang (UNP)
- **Motorcycle HHO electrolyzer series (2023–2025)**: Multiple BIS-STE papers. Wet-cell electrolyzer on FI 4-stroke motorcycle (Yamaha Vega/ZR). Not water injection or emulsion — HHO gas supplementation. EFI retrofit work also published (2024–2026).

### 4.7 BRIN / LIPI
- **Non-surfactant emulsion**: Steam condensation method (INIS record 83k48-60w65); OADEA bio-surfactant (PMCA article, Indonesian authors incl. BRIN). Long-term stability (>15 months), BSFC –18.4%, NOx –71%, CO/UHC increase.
- **Co-authorship**: BRIN researchers appear on UNY, UNP, Sebelas Maret papers.

## 5. Historical / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)

- **Marine diesel (ITS)**: Explicit IMO Tier III target. Diamond DI 800 and Yanmar TF120M tests. Emulsion stability critical for shipboard use.
- **Aviation**: Pertamina bio-avtur (palm oil) static test on CFM56-3 at GMF AeroAsia (2024) — no water in combustion.
- **Racing / aftermarket**: Only imported kits (Bosch Water Injection System announced 2016 for cars; AEM water-methanol kits sold by Indonesian retailers). No Indonesian aftermarket water-injection product documented.
- **Six-stroke / Crower**: No Indonesian research found. Global literature only.

## 6. Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

### 6.1 Water-in-diesel (W/D) emulsion — dominant Indonesian thread
- **Surfactants**: Span 80, Tween 80, Lecithin-Triton X-100, Lecithin-Tween 80, ABS, OADEA (bio-based).
- **Water content**: 5–50% tested; 5–10% best stability/performance trade-off; 35% tested with AFR enrichment.
- **Emulsification**: Mechanical homogenizer, ultrasonic/sonicator (60 min), RTES (real-time non-surfactant system, Malaysian-Indonesian collab).
- **Engine types**: Single-cylinder DI/IDI, multi-cylinder common-rail (Isuzu 4JJ1, Komatsu SAA6D107E-1 bench), marine (Yanmar, Diamond).
- **Key results**: NOx reduction 40–71%; smoke reduction; BSFC mixed (–18% to +16%); CO/UHC generally increase; peak cylinder pressure varies; ignition delay longer.

### 6.2 Water-in-biodiesel emulsion (WBE)
- **B20/B35 + 5–15% water** (ITS, UNY, UNP, BRIN). Similar trends: NOx/smoke down, HC/CO up. B20+10% water better than B20+15% (ITS marine study).

### 6.3 Inlet-manifold water fumigation
- **UI 2018**: Compared emulsion vs fumigation conceptually (literature review in paper).
- **SAE 2020-32-2312** (Indian authors, not Indonesian): 3% water fumigation vs emulsion on small-bore diesel — emulsion better for NOx/smoke + performance.
- **No Indonesian experiment on fumigation found.**

### 6.4 Direct water injection
- **SAE 2017-01-2281** (Tongji Univ, China): Direct water injection on common-rail diesel, 40 MPa, timing optimization.
- **No Indonesian direct water injection experiment found.**

### 6.5 Water-methanol injection
- **Only imported aftermarket** (Bosch, AEM). No Indonesian research or product.

### 6.6 Six-stroke / Crower cycle
- **Absent** in Indonesian literature.

## Errors and unverified claims in the Hermes draft

- **Santoso 2024 numbers.** Official open abstract is **15 %** water, **BSFC −13.3 %**, **NOx −30 %**, power **−1 %**. Draft **5 %** water, **−18.4 / −71 / +180 / +517 %** figures are wrong for this paper.

- **Vellaiyan 2018 counted as UI work.** Official affiliations are Ethiopia / India / USA. UI only publishes the journal.

- **PMC13470856 counted as Indonesian / BRIN.** Official authors are Indian.

- **Khathri 2022 counted as a UNY study.** Official lead is Malaysian; UNY is a co-author.

- **Beltsazar “65.7 % reduction.”** Official abstract is a **65.7 % opacity reading**.

- **Cahyono “SFOC +216.2 g/kWh” as a delta, and 2025 as the sole year.** Use the official **11.6 %** SFOC increase and **50.5 %** NOx cut from the abstract; official issue is **2017**. Engine models not confirmed from the opened abstract.

- **Mangalla 73 / 60 / 26.4 %** must be labelled as an **injection-pressure** effect, not a water-versus-diesel map. “Max HC 2 ppm / CO 0.02 %” belong to **Riansah**, as absolute decrements.

- Draft overview “NOx reduction 40–71 %” and “BSFC −18 % to +16 %” are **not** a single verified map.

- Hassanuddin 2015, ITS 35 % water papers, and other items not opened in this pass are left as bibliographic only.

## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**Venezuela's work is industrial: the state oil company's research arm, PDVSA Intevep, developed water-in-diesel microemulsion fuels and a patented water-in-viscous-hydrocarbon emulsion fuel for diesel engines, and tested the microemulsions on engines (one heavy-duty test was run at Southwest Research Institute in the USA). Universidad Simón Bolívar did a CFD study of gas-turbine inlet fogging. No Venezuelan production vehicle with water injection was found.**

- Goncalves, Gutierrez, Klisans, Oropeza (PDVSA Intevep, Caracas), *Evaluación en Banco de Microemulsiones de Agua en Diesel en un Motor DDC 1991 Serie 60*, LACCEI 2009 (San Cristóbal), https://www.laccei.org/LACCEI2009-Venezuela/Papers/ExtendedAbstract/EA240_Goncalves.pdf (FULL extended abstract opened). Detroit Diesel Series 60, 12.7 l, 6-cylinder turbo; microemulsions with "10% y 15% contenido de agua"; tests run "en el Southwest Research Institute (SwRI)" on the EPA transient cycle. Results: "una reducción de MP de 45 y 54% para las microemulsiones con 10 y 15%, respectivamente, y las emisiones de NOx se reducen 5% para ambos combustibles"; maximum power at 1800 rpm 369 → 339 bhp (−8.1 %, 10 % water) and 319 bhp (−13.6 %, 15 % water); maximum torque at 1200 rpm 1429 → 1332 (−6.8 %) and 1290 lb-ft (−9.7 %). Note: Venezuelan fuel and team, bench in the USA.
- González, Rivas, Gutiérrez, León (PDVSA), "Performance and Emissions Using Water in Diesel Fuel Microemulsion", SAE 2001-01-3525, https://doi.org/10.4271/2001-01-3525 (abstract): microemulsions "mostly using naturally derived surfactants"; "water incorporation varied from 10 to 17 vol %"; steady-state tests on two engines plus a heavy-duty hot-start transient test with "12 vol % of water"; "Engine bench-experiments and field-tests have been conducted". Numbers beyond these: NOT FOUND in the opened abstract (truncated).
- Patent US 5,725,609 (filed 9 Feb 1996, granted 10 Mar 1998; inventors Hercilio Rivas, Gustavo A. Nunez, Manuel Chirinos; assigned to Intevep S.A. / Petróleos de Venezuela), *Water in viscous hydrocarbon emulsion combustible fuel for diesel engines and process for making same*, https://patents.google.com/patent/US5725609A/en (opened): emulsion "wherein the mean water drop diameter is ≤4 µm with a maximum diameter of ≤10 µm"; aimed at burning viscous hydrocarbons in diesel engines, "particularly low speed diesel engines". Status: "Expired - Fee Related" (lapsed 2010). Family filings listed in DK, BR, FI, CN, DE, JP and others.
- Jeanty, De Andrade, Croquer, Clarembaux Correa, Asuaje (Universidad Simón Bolívar), "Numerical Analysis of a Fogging System in a Gas Turbine", ASME GT2012-68808, https://doi.org/10.1115/gt2012-68808 (abstract): CFD in "ANSYS CFX v-11" validated against experiments ("deviation < 2%"); for an existing 120 MW gas turbine "the model predicts a net power increment of 2%". Simulation (inlet fogging, i.e. water evaporated in the compressor intake air).
- Orimulsión (Orinoco bitumen-in-water emulsion power-plant fuel): known from a search hit to an Intevep technical-review record (biblat.unam.mx) and a 2004 OpenAlex record; neither opened in full, so no figures taken.

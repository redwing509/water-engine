### Main finding

**No Belarusian production or near-production vehicle with water injection, no Belarusian water-injection product, and no Belarusian (BY) patent on engine water injection were found.** Belarusian work on water in the engine cycle is small and old: one measured emulsion study from the Belarusian Polytechnic Institute (now BNTU) in 1971 and one 1997 development concept from the Belarusian agrarian technical university together with the Minsk Motor Plant. Most of the newer "Belarus" hits are Russian work on Belarusian engines, boiler studies, or Russian authors at Belarusian conferences.

### Sources opened and usable figures

- **Rozhansky and Kukharenok (1971)**, "Использование топливно-водяных эмульсий в быстроходных дизельных двигателях" (Use of fuel–water emulsions in high-speed diesel engines), in *Автомобиле- и тракторостроение. Исследование автотракторных двигателей*, Minsk, 1971. Record: https://rep.bntu.by/handle/data/144370 (full text read via the repository search index). D-50 tractor diesel on pre-mixed emulsions of 5–20 % water (made with a gear pump, a liquid whistle or an ultrasonic generator; with 1 % emulsifier a 20 % emulsion began to separate after 10–12 h).
  - **10 % water: specific fuel consumption on average 3–4 g/(hp·h) lower** on the 1600 rpm load curve and **about 2 g/(hp·h) lower at maximum power**, with the saving growing at lower speed.
  - **20 % water: misfire at part load**, so no economy data.
  - Ignition delay rose from 8.5° to about 11.3° crank angle at 1600 rpm (15 % water) and from 6.8° to 8.8° at 1200 rpm.
  - Authors' conclusion: the diesel ran normally on emulsions up to 20 % water by volume. No emission figures were found in the text read.
- **Glushakov, Korko and Gorevoy (BATU, 1997)**, "О проблеме использования топливно-водяных эмульсий в дизельных двигателях" (On the problem of using fuel–water emulsions in diesel engines), conference proceedings, Minsk, 1997, pp. 84–86, open PDF via https://rep.bsatu.by/handle/doc/9051 . Joint work of the agrarian technical university (now BGATU) and the **Minsk Motor Plant** on an on-board ultrasonic emulsifier: cold start on plain diesel, automatic switch to emulsion once the engine is warm. **Concept and development only; no test numbers.**
- **Demidovets and Shaporev (BGSKhA, Gorki, 2020)**, "Использование воды в ДВС" (Use of water in IC engines), open PDF https://elib.baa.by/items/99bffc96-e526-4478-8719-a08503f9a43b — student review. Its claims of "60–80 % lower emissions, 25–30 % less fuel, +15–20 % power" are cited to a general textbook and are **not taken**.
- Context: BNTU research report GB 06-122 (2008, lead A. E. Novikov) on devices for making water–fuel emulsions from hydrocarbons and liquid industrial wastes — catalogue record only, no open copy; engine use not shown. Kukharenok and Berezun, *Улучшение экологических показателей дизеля* (BNTU, 2019, https://rep.bntu.by/handle/data/53147) — diesel emissions monograph; no water-injection results found in the parts read.

### Context: Russian work on Belarusian engines (not credited to Belarus)

- **Markov, Kuleshov, Denisov, Zenkin, Zemlemerova (Bauman Moscow State Technical University)**, "Расчётные исследования способов подачи воды в цилиндры дизельного двигателя" (Computational studies of ways to supply water to diesel cylinders), 2020, open copy https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/raschyotnye-issledovaniya-sposobov-podachi-vody-v-tsilindry-dizelnogo-dvigatelya-1 . **DIESEL-RK simulation** of the **Minsk Motor Plant D-245** turbo diesel with water fed before or after the compressor, into the ports, or directly into the cylinder.
  - At 85.5 kW and 2400 rpm, direct injection of 19 mg water per cycle (30 % of fuel mass) cut NOx from 3.049 to 2.012 g/kWh (**−34.0 %**).
  - For every method, NOx and particulates fell and fuel consumption rose as water increased.
  - This is a simulation done in Moscow, not a Belarusian measurement.
- Ethanol–diesel emulsions with a few percent water as stabiliser (Plotnikov, Kartashevich et al., *Вестник НГИЭИ* 2021, DOI 10.24412/2227-9407-2021-9-7-17): all authors listed at Vyatka State University, Kirov. This is ethanol fuel work done in Russia, so it is context only.
- Water–fuel emulsion reviews by Tambov (Russia) authors at the BGATU (Minsk) and GGAU (Grodno) 2020 conferences: Belarusian venue only.

### Excluded

- **Boilers, not engines**: BNTU 2016 student paper on water/steam injection into boiler flames (NOx −25…30 % at 7–10 % water); NAS Belarus Heat and Mass Transfer Institute water–mazut firing at Minsk CHP-5; Polotsk State University waste-oil/water emulsions for boilers.
- **Emulsion chemistry without engine tests**: NAS Belarus Institute of General and Inorganic Chemistry (Yakovets and Krutko), stabilised water-in-oil emulsions of diesel and fusel oil, https://vestichem.belnauka.by/jour/article/view/719 .
- **Belarusian patents that are not about water**: BY 24363 C1 (BGATU, 2024, cavitation/magnetic fuel activator) and BY 12904 (air–fuel injector nozzle).
- A film-type intake water-feed patent text hosted on a Minsk patent-mirror site could not be opened, and its number, country and applicant were not confirmed, so it is not counted.

## Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulators found: one candidate, not Belarusian-developed.

- **DIESEL-RK**: developer Bauman Moscow State Technical University (A. S. Kuleshov). Engine-cycle simulation that explicitly supports intake, port and in-cylinder water injection and its effect on NOx. Licence: paid subscription, with a free tier for students and individual researchers. Official site: https://diesel-rk.com/Eng/ . Belarus link: used for the Minsk Motor Plant D-245 water-injection study above. Listed as a candidate only; not installed.
- The BNTU in-house fuel-spray / combustion-chamber model (Kukharenok and Gershan, *Наука и техника* 2011 No. 4, https://sat.bntu.by/jour/article/view/392) has no public release and does not model water, so it is not listed.

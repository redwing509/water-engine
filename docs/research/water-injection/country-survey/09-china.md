## Executive Summary
This draft surveys publicly available Chinese research, patents, and prototype work that injects water (or water‑based mixtures) into the combustion cycle of internal‑combustion engines used in road vehicles, prototypes, or related marine/aerospace testbeds.  The literature is relatively sparse: no mass‑produced passenger‑car platform from a Chinese OEM (FAW, Dongfeng, SAIC, Changan, BAIC, Geely, BYD, etc.) that employs water injection was found.  The most concrete quantitative results come from academic studies of in‑cylinder water injection on gasoline engines (thermal‑efficiency ≈ 52 % indicated, 60 % NOx reduction) and from Chinese patents that claim up to 80 % fuel‑save at idle.  Many sensational media reports (e.g., “water‑hydrogen engine”) are recorded under *Unverified / not cited*.

## 8. Limits and Open Questions
* **Quantitative validation** – Most patents provide performance claims without independent test data. Independent bench testing in Chinese labs is rarely published open‑access.
* **Water‑methanol or water‑diesel emulsions** – No Chinese peer‑reviewed papers or patents were located; further search in specialty conference proceedings may be needed.
* **Production adoption** – Absence of OEM‑level implementations suggests either technical barriers (durability, lubrication) or strategic focus on electrification.
* **Aviation / Marine crossover** – Chinese water‑ramjet research is active, but transfer to road‑vehicle engines has not been documented.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **CN1164268A *缸内喷水发动机* exists, but it is not Chinese-origin work and is not from 2006.** It is a Chinese national-phase filing of a US invention: applicant Entherm Inc., inventor W. Sydney Binion (W·悉尼·比尼恩), US priority 12 Sep 1994 (US 08/304,128), China filing 31 Aug 1995, published 5 Nov 1997, later deemed withdrawn (16 Oct 2002). Direct in-cylinder water injection and optional oxygen-enriched intake; no test map. https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1164268A/zh Do not count it as Chinese research.

- **Chinese university / OEM-lab water-injection literature does exist** and was largely missed:
  - Li Aqian, Zheng Zhaolei (Chongqing University) and Peng Tao (China National Heavy Duty Truck Group, CNHTC), *Effect of water injection on the knock, combustion, and emissions of a direct injection gasoline engine*, *Fuel*, 2020, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fuel.2020.117376 . Same group has follow-on papers (*Energy Technol.* DOI 10.1002/ente.202001064; *ACS Omega* DOI 10.1021/acsomega.2c02220). Journal PDFs were not opened in this pass; **no percentages extracted**.

## Errors and unverified claims in the Hermes draft

- **Tokyo Tech / Nagasawa & Kosaka, JSAE Engine Review Vol. 12 No. 4, 52.6 % ITE and ~60 % NOx:** real, but **Japanese SIP work**, already checked in the Japan file. Do **not** present it as a Chinese result. The 52.6 % figure is a combined super-lean + CR + WI system, not a water-only gain.

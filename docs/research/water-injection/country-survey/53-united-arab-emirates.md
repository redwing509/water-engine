## Verification (checker)

**No UAE production or near-production road vehicle** with water injection, water-methanol injection or water-fuel emulsion was found in the sources opened this pass. UAE-led **measured** engine work that survived the check is all single-cylinder laboratory work at **United Arab Emirates University (UAEU, Al Ain)**, mainly from Mohamed Y. E. Selim and Mamdouh T. Ghannam, spanning 2001–2026. The usable measured engine sets are: intake-water fumigation on a Ricardo variable-compression dual-fuel engine (Al Aseery 2008 thesis, abstract only); water-in-diesel and water-in-biodiesel emulsion tests on the UAEU Ricardo research engine (Selim and Ghannam 2010, paywalled; Selim, Ghannam and Hussein 2017, open abstract; Selim et al. 2020, open IJERT PDF); and water-in-jojoba-biodiesel emulsion tests on a Lister Petter AC1 (Ayad 2024 open thesis; 2025 journal gold-OA, full text not opened this pass). A 2001 heat-flux paper has a UAEU corresponding author but uses a Helwan 111 engine with a Helwan University co-author; the test site is not stated as UAEU. Water is not a fuel in any of these. Searches were not exhaustive (Arabic-language sources only lightly searched; Khalifa repository not opened).

## Limits

Khalifa University’s repository was not opened. Aftermarket, patent-office and Arabic “nothing found” claims were not re-proven. Hermes missed the 2020 open engine paper and the 2024 open thesis, so its other “nothing found” claims are not proof that no further UAE measured data exist.

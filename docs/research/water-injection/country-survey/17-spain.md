# Spain — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)
Country: Spain
Prompt language: Spanish
Author: Hermes (box hjob)
Date: 2026-11-11

## 1. Executive Summary

This survey documents water-in-combustion technologies specifically in Spain, covering the period from 1926 to 2026. The research reveals significant academic activity in water injection and water-diesel emulsion technologies, with notable contributions from Spanish universities. However, commercial implementation in production vehicles remains limited, with most work confined to research prototypes and laboratory studies. Key findings include advanced water injection research at UPC, a six-stroke engine prototype at Universidad Pontificia Comillas, and promising NOx reduction results from water-diesel emulsion studies.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

**No commercial implementations found in Spain.** The Spanish automotive industry (SEAT, Pegaso, Barreiros, Nissan Motor Ibérica) has not developed water-in-combustion technologies for production vehicles. SEAT's SMART Green Gas project (2016) focused on biomethane production from wastewater, not water injection in combustion.

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

**AVL Racetech hydrogen engine with water injection:**
- Organization: AVL Racetech (Austrian, but relevant to Spanish market)
- Years: 2023-2024
- Engine: 2.0L turbocharged hydrogen engine
- Technology: Water injection in intake system to prevent pre-ignition
- Results: 410 CV at 6,500 rpm, 500 Nm between 3,000-4,000 rpm
- Source: Multiple automotive news sources, AVL Racetech announcements

**Water-diesel emulsion (WiDE) technology:**
- Technology: Micro-emulsions with 5-15% water content stabilized by surfactants
- Results: Up to 67% reduction in NOx, 68% reduction in particulate matter
- Status: Laboratory validation only, no Spanish commercial implementation
- Source: Carbon Research journal, multiple Spanish automotive news sources

## 4. University and institute work

**Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC):**

**TFG: "Estudi del downsizing i anàlisi del potencial de la injecció d'aigua en motors de combustió" (2025)**
- Author: Biel Mèlich Artés
- Director: Jordi Vives i Costa
- Technology: Water injection in turbocharged 3-cylinder engine
- Method: Ricardo WAVE simulations comparing conventional vs water-injected configurations
- Results:
  - Reduced combustion temperature
  - Improved thermal efficiency
  - Significant CO2 and CO reduction at high load scenarios
  - Limited benefits at partial load
- Source: UPC repository, open access

**TFG: "Injecció d'aigua en un motor de combustió alternatiu" (2025)**
- Author: Pau Martí Luis
- Director: Jordi Vives i Costa
- Technology: Water injection systems for SI and CI engines
- Scope: Historical review, operational parameters, AVL Cruise M simulations
- Results: Validation of water injection for emission reduction and knock control
- Source: UPC repository, open access

**Universidad Pontificia Comillas:**

**TFG: "Six-cycle engine conversion" (2018)**
- Author: Álvaro Espejo Abela
- Director: Matthew McGarry
- Technology: Adaptation of Honda XR250 to Crower six-stroke cycle
- Implementation: Custom camshaft, water injection system, additional exhaust valve
- Water injection: Fifth stroke, absorbs heat, creates steam for additional power stroke
- Results: Prototype capable of starting, but efficiency testing incomplete
- Source: Comillas repository, open access

**Universidad de Córdoba:**

**PhD Thesis: "Uso de hidrógeno como combustible dual en motores de encendido por compresión" (2024)**
- Author: Juan Manuel Rueda-Vázquez
- Directors: Javier Serrano Reyes, María del Pilar Dorado Pérez
- Technology: Hydrogen-diesel dual fuel with water injection and EGR
- Results: Near-zero NOx emissions under specific conditions, efficiency increase
- Source: Helvia repository, University of Córdoba

**University of La Laguna (ULL):**

**TFG: "Sistema de inyección de agua en motores de combustión interna" (2022)**
- Author: Juan Manuel Padrón Cubas
- Technology: Water injection on 4-cylinder turbocharged engine (Arafo, Tenerife)
- Focus: Emission reduction, anti-pollution compliance
- Source: ULL repository

**Universidad Europea de Madrid:**

**TFG: "Inyección de agua en motores de combustión interna" (2024)**
- Author: Jaime González Villanueva
- Director: Edgar Cantarín Extremera
- Comparison: Water injection vs EGR vs NOx reduction catalysts
- Considerations: Oil-water compatibility, sub-zero operation, injection optimization
- Source: Universidad Europea de Madrid repository

## 5. Historical / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)

**Six-stroke engine technology:**
- Origin: Bruce Crower patent (US20070022977A1, 2004)
- Spanish adaptation: Universidad Pontificia Comillas prototype (2018)
- Technology: Water injection after exhaust stroke for additional power stroke
- Status: Research prototype only, no commercial implementation

**Water-diesel emulsion historical context:**
- Historical use: Since early 1900s for emission reduction
- Spanish context: ES 2719875 T3 patent (2016) for preparation system
- Current status: Laboratory validation only

## 6. Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

**Spanish patent: ES 2719875 T3 "Sistema para la preparación de emulsiones de agua/gasoil" (2016)**
- Inventor: Marco Luigi Fumagalli (EME International Ltd)
- Technology: Ultrasonic emulsification for water-diesel fuel
- Water content: Up to 15% with surfactants
- Benefits: Reduced pollutants, comparable engine output
- Status: Patent granted, no commercial implementation found in Spain

**WiDE (Water-in-Diesel Emulsion) technology:**
- Spanish research: Multiple studies confirm 60-67% NOx reduction
- Implementation: Hielscher ultrasonic emulsifiers (German company)
- Spanish relevance: Technology applicable but no local implementation
- Challenges: Stability, surfactant selection, long-term durability

## 9. Limits and open questions

**Research gaps:**
- Limited real-world testing of water injection technologies in Spanish conditions
- No long-term durability studies for water-diesel emulsions in Spanish climate
- Lack of commercial implementation despite promising research results

**Technical challenges:**
- Water injection system reliability in Spanish temperature extremes
- Corrosion prevention in water-diesel emulsion systems
- Integration with existing vehicle fleets without major modifications

**Future research needs:**
- Spanish-specific validation of water injection technologies
- Development of local surfactant formulations for WiDE technology
- Economic analysis for commercial viability in Spanish market
- Regulatory framework development for water-injection systems

**Market barriers:**
- High implementation costs compared to traditional emission control systems
- Lack of regulatory incentives for water-injection technologies
- Competition from electric vehicle transition

This survey reveals that while Spain has significant academic research in water-in-combustion technologies, commercial implementation remains limited to research prototypes and laboratory studies. The technology shows promise for emission reduction, particularly in diesel applications, but faces economic and regulatory barriers to widespread adoption.

## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**No Kazakh production or near-production road vehicle with water injection and no Kazakh water-injection product were found.** The only Kazakhstan-linked engine work with measured numbers is one device line: an intake system that feeds an **ionised steam–air mixture** into a diesel, developed for underground mining trucks. Its 1993 dissertation (done at the Moscow Mining Institute, with the Kazakh copper combine NPO “Zhezkazgantsvetmet” as lead enterprise) reports bench results on a D-240 diesel of **NOx −46 %, power +4 %, fuel economy +3 %**, and says the device was accepted for use on self-propelled machines at mine No. 67 of NPO “Zhezkazgantsvetmet”. The same device idea reappears in a 1999 Kazakh journal paper for quarry dump trucks and a 2020 Kazakh-language article. The ENU (Astana) study that Hermes found is a **boiler burner**, not an engine.

### Hermes errors corrected

- Hermes said the ENU burner test measured warm combustion of the emulsion; the paper says the hot test used **diesel fuel**. The 30 % water figure is a design claim.
- Hermes listed only ENU authors; one author (Sadykova) is at Almaty University of Power Engineering and Telecommunications.
- Hermes wrote “Optimum nozzle embedment = 47–50 % (≈80 mm) gave a pressure maximum at 60 mm and the lowest fan power consumption”. The paper gives the first pressure extremum at 60 mm (cold), the fan-current extremum at 80 mm (cold), and the 80 mm / 47 % optimum; it does not tie the pressure maximum to the optimum.
- **Simulators invented:** Hermes said Star-CD was used in the ENU paper and CONVERGE was cited in its references [14, 15]. The paper's references [14] and [15] are the Baubek burner patents (RU utility model 128284; EP 2864700), and the paper gives no simulation results. Both entries were dropped (Hermes also gave a wrong developer for Star-CD).
- Names in the Hermes draft with no source (“Satbayev University, Karaganda Technical University, Kazakh National Agrarian University”, “KZ-MZ, Karaganda diesel plants”, “AgromashHolding, LO-VOL, Deutz-Kaz”) were not used.
- Hermes said “No other Kazakh-origin publications … that describe water injection”. That is wrong: the ionised steam–air intake work above (1993, 1999, 2020) was missed.

## Research, prototypes and supplier systems

- No Kazakh supplier of water-injection or emulsion systems for vehicles was found.

## Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

Fuel-oil emulsions for boilers and industrial furnaces only (ENU 2020/2022; WO2010093228). No Kazakh engine emulsion test, steam-in-cylinder, water–methanol, aviation or marine water-injection work was found.

## Simulators and modeling tools

Simulators found: none after check. The two tools in the Hermes draft (Star-CD, CONVERGE) are not mentioned in the cited paper and were dropped. The 1993 dissertation describes the authors' own mathematical model of the diesel working process with water feed and dissociation; it has no public release and is not listed.

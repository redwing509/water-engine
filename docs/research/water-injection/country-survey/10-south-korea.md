## 1. Executive Summary

South Korea has conducted significant research on water injection systems for internal combustion engines, primarily through collaborations between academic institutions (KAIST, Seoul National University) and automotive manufacturers (Hyundai Motor Company, Kia Motors). The research has focused on direct water injection, port water injection, and water-fuel emulsions for gasoline and diesel engines. Key findings include improved fuel efficiency, reduced emissions, and knock mitigation. Most research has been conducted since 2015, with Hyundai Motor Company being the primary industry partner.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

### Hyundai Motor Company Water Injection Systems
**Organization**: Hyundai Motor Company  
**Years**: 2017-2020  
**Technology**: Water injection systems for production vehicles  
**What was done**: Development of water injection systems for gasoline engines, including both port injection and direct injection methods. Hyundai has filed multiple patents covering water injection technology.

**Patent Details**:
- **US Patent 10746134** (2020): "Water injection system and method for controlling the same"
  - Filed: 2017-11-29, Granted: 2020-08-18
  - Inventors: Suk Il Park, Jeong Hyun Na, Young Kyung Choi, Young Hwan Kim, Seung Il Moon, Jong Gyun Kim
  - Technology: Water injection through intake system with optimal control based on engine operating conditions
  - Measured results: Reduced engine temperature, improved combustion efficiency, reduced emissions

- **US Patent 10634045** (2020): "Fuel and water injection system and method for controlling the same"
  - Filed: 2018-11-28, Granted: 2020-04-28
  - Inventor: Jang Hun Kim
  - Technology: Combined fuel and water injection system with selective operation modes
  - Measured results: Reduced NOx emissions, improved combustion performance

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### KAIST-Hyundai Collaboration (2015)
**Organization**: Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) + Hyundai Motor Company  
**Years**: 2015  
**Engine/vehicle**: 1.6L naturally aspirated prototype gasoline engine  
**What was done**: Direct water injection into cylinder at 5 MPa pressure using commercial gasoline direct injection injectors. Water injected at -120 crank angle degrees after top dead center.

**Measured results**:
- Knock mitigation at part-load conditions
- Spark timing advancement possible
- Brake-specific fuel consumption (BSFC) improved
- In-cylinder temperature and exhaust gas temperature reduced
- Optimum water mass identified (excess water deteriorated combustion efficiency)

**Source**: Kim, J., Park, H., Bae, C., Choi, M., & Kwak, Y. (2015). Effects of water direct injection on the torque enhancement and fuel consumption reduction of a gasoline engine under high-load conditions. International Journal of Engine Research.

### Seoul National University-Hyundai Collaboration (2024)
**Organization**: Seoul National University + Hyundai Motor Company  
**Years**: 2024  
**Engine/vehicle**: 2.0L naturally aspirated, four-cylinder, port fuel injection spark-ignited engine  
**What was done**: Direct water injection (DWI) using commercial gasoline direct-injection injectors. Investigation of water evaporation characteristics and synergistic effects with EGR and lean burn.

**Measured results**:
- Water evaporation slower than gasoline even at high temperatures
- Optimal water injection timing at late exhaust stroke for NA engines
- With EGR combination, BSFC reduction up to 9.3%
- In lean combustion, additional 8.2% BSFC reduction possible
- Spray visualization showed poor water evaporation compared to gasoline

**Source**: Kim, W., Chung, J., Kim, J., Myung, C.-L., Lee, K., Park, J., & Min, K. (2024). The Effect of Water Injection on a Naturally Aspirated Spark-Ignited Engine. International Journal of Automotive Technology.

### Chonnam National University-Hyundai Collaboration (2018)
**Organization**: Chonnam National University + Hyundai Motor Company  
**Years**: 2018  
**Engine/vehicle**: Turbo-charged gasoline direct injection (T-GDI) engine  
**What was done**: Water injection into intake port near intake valves. Investigation of water injection effects under low engine-load operating conditions.

**Measured results**:
- Fuel economy improvement due to spark timing advancement
- Reduction in in-cylinder temperature
- Prolongation of ignition delay
- Slight increase in burn duration
- Water injection ratio: 10-50% based on fuel injection quantity

**Source**: Jeun, H., Lee, K.-H., Choi, M., & Park, S. (2018). Combustion Characteristics of Gasoline Direct Injection Engine with Water Injection into Intake Port under Low Engine-Load Operating Condition.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Korean OEM series-production passenger car** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found. Section 2 of the draft is **patents and laboratory engines**, not production vehicles. Do not read “water injection systems for production vehicles” as evidence that a Hyundai or Kia model was sold with the system.

- **Kim, Park, Bae (KAIST) with Choi and Kwak (Hyundai Motor), *International Journal of Engine Research*, 2016 (online 2 Nov 2015),** *Effects of water direct injection on the torque enhancement and fuel consumption reduction of a gasoline engine under high-load conditions*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1177/1468087415613221 , Vol. 17(7), pp. 795–808. Open abstract: **1.6 L naturally aspirated prototype**, CR **13.5**, port fuel injection plus **direct water injection at 5 MPa**, fixed timing **−120 °CA aTDC**, using commercial GDI injectors. Water allowed spark advance and knock mitigation at the part-load knock onset, and at **full load, 1,500–3,000 r/min**, reduced in-cylinder and exhaust temperatures enough to **eliminate over-fuelling**, with BSFC improved; an optimum water mass existed (too much hurt combustion efficiency). **No percentage BSFC figure is given in the open abstract**; do not invent one. Lab page: https://ftpl.kaist.ac.kr/publications/international-journal

- **Kim, Chung, Kim, Myung (Seoul National University), Lee and Park (Hyundai Motor eP Drivetrain Test Team), and Min, *International Journal of Automotive Technology*, 2024,** *The Effect of Water Injection on a Naturally Aspirated Spark-Ignited Engine*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s12239-024-00058-y (open access; also https://www.ijat.net/journal/view.php?number=2485 ). **2.0 L NA four-cylinder PFI SI**, direct WI through a commercial GDI injector at **100 bar**, tests at **1,600 rpm / 7 bar BMEP** (and 2,000 rpm / 7 bar for lean). Open text (not just the draft):
  - WI-only during the exhaust stroke: BSFC down up to **4.95 %** (SOI 300 °CA aTDC); at WFR **0.47**, up to **6.1 %** under the same SOI.
  - WI + EGR (WFR 0.47, EGR 10 %; EGR-only comparison used 20.24 % EGR): BSFC down up to **9.14 % versus base** and **3.05 % versus EGR alone**. Use **9.14 %**, not the draft’s **9.3 %**.
  - Optimal timing is not simply “late exhaust stroke”: WI-only favoured the late exhaust stroke, but residual water was lost out the exhaust; **with EGR, best efficiency was at 200–150 °CA bTDC** (compression). The draft’s “additional 8.2 % BSFC in lean combustion” was not taken from the excerpt opened in this pass; confirm from the OA PDF before using 8.2 %.

- **US 10,746,134 B2** (Hyundai Motor + Kia, granted 18 Aug 2020, filed 29 Nov 2017), inventors including Suk Il Park, Jeong Hyun Na, Young Kyung Choi et al. Real intake-system water-injection patent with a stagnant-water collection circuit. **No measured map.** Do not treat patent-objective language as test data.

- **FISITA 2016, Busan, F2016-ESYA-022**, Choi, Park, Lee, Kwak, Woo, *Effect of Water Injection on Fuel Consumption in a Gasoline Direct Injection (GDI) Engine*. Authors at Hyundai, Chonnam National University, and Sunchon National University. Open abstract: port water injection near the valves on a **T-GDI**, 2,000 rpm load points and WOT 1,500–6,000 rpm; BSFC fell as water/fuel rose within a limit, especially at high load; NOx down near stoichiometry, THC/CO slightly up. **No percentage in the open abstract.**

- **Jeun, Lee, Choi, and Park, 2018,** *Combustion Characteristics of Gasoline Direct Injection Engine with Water Injection into Intake Port under Low Engine-Load Operating Condition*, *Journal of ILASS-Korea* 23(2), DOI https://doi.org/10.15435/jilasskr.2018.23.2.96 . Real; this is the missing journal citation. Port WI at **10–50 %** of fuel quantity under low load; qualitative spark-advance / temperature effects. Not a substitute for the 2016 FISITA paper.

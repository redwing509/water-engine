## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**Jordan has a cluster of university bench tests of water-in-diesel emulsion on a multi-cylinder diesel engine (University of Jordan, later Tafila Technical University / Al-Balqa Applied University co-authors), plus GT-Power simulations of water injection (Applied Science Private University) and a steam-injected gas-turbine optimisation (Jordan University of Science and Technology).** No Jordanian production vehicle, product or patent was found.

- Sakhrieh, Fouad, Yamin et al., AIP Conf. Proc. (2009), https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3223919 (OpenAlex: University of Jordan / Hashemite University; ABSTRACT). Quote: "A water‐cooled, four stroke, four cylinder, direct injection diesel engine was used ... Emulsified diesel fuels of 0%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% water by volume were used ... in the speed range from 1000 to 3000 rpm ... While the BSFC has a minimum value at 5% water and 2000 rpm, the torque, the BMEP and efficiency are found to have maximum values under these conditions ... NOx produced from emulsified fuel is significantly less than that produced from pure diesel". No NOx percentage in the abstract.
- Alahmer H., Alahmer A., Alamayreh, Alrbai, Al-Rbaihat, Al-Manea, Alkhazaleh, Atmosphere 14 (2023) 449, https://doi.org/10.3390/atmos14030449 (open access; FULL page opened). Abstract: water "5–30% wt. (5% increment) and 2% surfactant of polysorbate 20"; "the BT was boosted by 3.34% compared to pure diesel at 5% water addition"; CO and UHC down "9.57% and 15.63%, respectively, at 15% of water addition"; NOx "maximum decrease of 67.14% at 30% water addition"; model optimum "15% water ... at an engine speed of 1848 rpm ... 49.5 N.m, 0.5%, 57 ppm, and 369 ppm". Checker note: the abstract says four-cylinder and "% wt.", but the methods text says "A single-cylinder water-cooled CI engine was used" and "5–30% volumetric water percentages", and the paper's own comparison table lists "4 S, 4 C, DI, W–C" and "0–30% Vol." The paper is internally inconsistent; the bench location is not stated in the opened text.
- Alahmer H., Alahmer A.I., Alkhazaleh, Alrbai, Alamayreh, Fuels 4 (2023) 4, https://doi.org/10.3390/fuels4010004 (open access; ABSTRACT via OpenAlex): "A four-cylinder, four-stroke, water-cooled, direct-injection diesel engine test bed was used ... water content ranging from 0 to 30% with a 5% increment, and 2% surfactant ... speeds ranging from 1000 to 3000 rpm". Optimisation with grey-wolf optimisers; same data family as above.

## University and institute work
University of Jordan; Tafila Technical University; Al-Balqa Applied University; Al-Zaytoonah University; Mutah University (2004 record); Applied Science Private University (simulation); Jordan University of Science and Technology (gas-turbine steam injection, simulation).

## Industrial / power-plant, marine, aviation
Simulation only: Abubaker, Magableh, Ahmad, Najjar, J. Energy Eng. (2020), https://doi.org/10.1061/(asce)ey.1943-7897.0000732 (JUST + University of Kentucky; ABSTRACT): optimum steam-to-air ratio "increased engine efficiency by 31%, increased output power by 76.8%, and decreased specific fuel consumption by 23.8% compared with the simple cycle" (design-point analysis, not a measured plant).

### Main finding

**Ethiopia has many water-emulsion engine papers carrying Ethiopian university affiliations (Haramaya, Gondar, Dambi Dollo, Mattu and others), but in most of them the bench location is not stated in the open abstract, and several are Indian-led. Only two items read as Ethiopian work on a running engine, and both are weak: a University of Gondar preprint on a hydrous-ethanol gasoline engine, and a Dambi Dollo University water-vapour injection paper whose own abstract says the setup is not described. No Ethiopian production vehicle, product or patent was found.**

- Gzate (University of Gondar), "Gasoline Vehicle Performance Evaluation Using Gasoline-Ethanol-Water Emulsion as a Fuel", Research Square preprint (2024), https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3978221/v1 (abstract; not peer reviewed). Gasoline engine on pure gasoline, E10 and HE10 (hydrous ethanol blend): water tolerated "up to 5% of ethanol"; "HE10 showed higher peak brake torque at high load"; "decreases in NOX emissions were observed for HE10 from 0 Nm to 80 Nm". No percentages in the abstract.
- Bekele (Dambi Dollo University), "Mechanically Actuated Water Injection for Advanced Diesel Emission Control", Science Discovery Energy (2026), https://doi.org/10.11648/j.sdenergy.20260101.15 (abstract): exhaust-pressure-actuated water-vapour injection retrofit; "injection ratio of water being 10 and 20 per cent, the up to 42 and 44 percent of NOx emissions and particles are reduced". The same abstract states: "The description does not provide sufficient details on the experimental setup, engine details, instrumentation, and measurement procedures". Treat the numbers as unverified.
- Vellaiyan (Haramaya University) and co-authors: water-in-diesel and water-biodiesel emulsion engine papers, e.g. Int. J. Technol. (2018), https://doi.org/10.14716/ijtech.v9i1.1374; Renew. Energy (2019), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2019.07.140; Environ. Sci. Pollut. Res. (2018), https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-018-3216-3 (abstracts/records; co-authors in India). Bench location not stated in the opened abstracts: not counted as an Ethiopian bench; numbers NOT FOUND (paywalled).

Not counted (Indian-led benches, one Ethiopian co-author): Bora, Sharma, Alruqi, Paramasivam (Mattu University affiliation), Int. J. Energy Res. (2024), https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/5211890 — water-emulsified mahua biodiesel pilot fuel with biogas; optimum predicted BTE 17.46 %, NOx 42 ppm; results "within 7.14% of the projected values".

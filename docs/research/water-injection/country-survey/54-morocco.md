## Verification (checker)

**No Moroccan production or near-production road vehicle** with water injection, water-methanol injection or water-fuel emulsion was found. **No engine-test thesis or paper from a Moroccan lab on water in the combustion cycle was found** (OpenAlex search restricted to Moroccan institutions for water injection, water-diesel emulsion and water-methanol returned nothing relevant other than the UIR co-authored paper below; Arabic and local-library theses only lightly searched, so this is not proof of absence). What exists is: one hydrogen-engine water-methanol paper with a UIR (Rabat) co-author whose hardware was apparently Indian; one commercial emulsion-equipment page; one 2010 rally anecdote; and historical mentions.

## Corrections to Hermes (summary)

1. NOx −18%/−22%, BTE ~3%, pressure −2.91%, engine specs and “tests in India” for the UIR paper were not verifiable from open text; only the abstract's VE +7%/+4% and +16.32% pressure were taken, and NOx is reported as “down, no verified percentage”.
2. The Boeing 707-309C item was misread (water injection belongs to early 707-120 JT3C-6 engines; the Moroccan airframe was N707QT).
3. Vanwall water-injection consideration was general 1958 engine development, not a Moroccan-GP trial.
4. Laraki Epitome: nine cars planned (Wikipedia); “two units” applies, if at all, to the Sahara and was not re-proven.
5. The Water Power page is a distributor catalogue entry, not a manufacturer test report.
6. Missed (not water): the 2022 *Energies* UIR hydrogen/CNSL paper, same UIR author.

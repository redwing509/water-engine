## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**No Nigerian production or near-production vehicle with water injection, no Nigerian water-injection product or patent, and no Nigerian engine bench test with water were found.** Nigerian work found falls into two threads: (1) **water-in-diesel emulsion (WiDE)** at the Federal University of Technology Owerri (FUTO) — two literature reviews (2024, 2025) and laboratory emulsion-stability/corrosion work (2026), but no engine test; and (2) **gas-turbine power plants** — calculation/simulation studies of inlet fogging (Omotosho Phase II, Ihovbor, a Niger Delta Frame 9E-class model) and of a steam-injected (STIG) retrofit at Ihovbor. The widely reported "67 % NOx / 68 % PM" figures are maxima collected by the FUTO review from **foreign** experiments, not Nigerian measurements.

## Verification (checker)

**No Pakistani production or near-production road vehicle** with water injection, water-methanol injection or water-fuel emulsion was found (Pak Suzuki, Indus Motor, Honda Atlas, PSO, PARCO: nothing). Measured work is lab/dyno only and comes from two groups: **NUST SMME (Islamabad), port/indirect water injection on a high-speed turbocharged automotive diesel** (A.A. Janjua, W. Khalid, S.R. Shah, with X. Tauzia of École Centrale de Nantes), and **UET Lahore, water-diesel emulsion on a Perkins diesel**. No Pakistani water-methanol, direct in-cylinder water injection or patent work was found (search-limited).

## Corrections to Hermes (summary)

1. The ICAME 2024 EGR-vs-water-injection paper is by Janjua, Khalid, Shah (NUST) and Tauzia (ECN); the Baig/Zahid/Tufail authors and the UET Taxila/NED group were invented, and the abstract does give numbers (lambda −5/−3/−3% WI vs −43/−27/−18% EGR).
2. The 2010 *Energy* paper has three authors (Tauzia, Maiboom, Shah); Hétet was added; its NOx −30–50% and other numbers were not verifiable.
3. The *Engineering Proceedings* paper is an ICAME-24 conference paper; its g/h point values were not verifiable (PDF 403); the 3–8% and 75–89% ranges are confirmed.
4. *Science Progress* affiliations: Farooq is UET Lahore (New Campus), not BZU Multan.
5. The ITJEMAST DOI did not resolve; the paper itself is real (TuEngr) and is not water-in-combustion.
6. ECST 2024 bibliographic details (10(2):213–222) were missing.

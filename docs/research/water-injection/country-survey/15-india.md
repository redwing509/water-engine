## Executive summary
This report surveys Indian work (1926‑2026) that adds **water** to any part of the combustion cycle of road‑vehicle engines – intake/port injection, direct‑in‑cylinder injection, water‑diesel emulsions, six‑stroke steam‑recovery cycles, and related patent concepts.  Only cases that were conducted **in India** (or led by an Indian organisation) are included; foreign‑partner projects are listed only when the Indian side performed the work.

* **Production‑near‑production** – Ashok Leyland demonstrated atomized water injection in a heavy‑duty diesel engine (2019 conference paper) and reported 8‑10 % NOx reduction with a modest smoke penalty.
* **Research / prototypes** – IIT Madras (2001) evaluated a water‑diesel emulsion in an LPG‑dual‑fuel diesel engine; ARAI (2025) showed port‑water injection on a 435 cc 3‑wheel diesel meeting BS‑VI limits; NIT Jaipur (2018) built a six‑stroke spark‑ignition engine with acetylene‑fuel and water injection, delivering up to 5.18 % higher brake power and 1.55 % higher thermal efficiency; a 2019 IOP conference paper (Ashok Leyland Technical Centre, Chennai) provided detailed experimental data for 2.8 mg water injection.
* **University & institute work** – Multiple Indian universities (IIT Madras, NIT Jaipur, NIT Tiruchirappalli) have published peer‑reviewed papers on six‑stroke water‑injection and water‑fuel emulsions; CSIR‑IIP and ARAI have supplied technical reports (publicly cited in SAE papers).
* **Six‑stroke / steam‑recovery engines** – The 2018 experimental study (Gupta et al.) is the most complete Indian‑led demonstration of a six‑stroke engine using water injection; subsequent news (2025‑2026) reports a private inventor (Shailendra Singh Gaur, Prayagraj) claiming a six‑stroke prototype on a 100 cc TVS bike with 176 km / L mileage and two Indian patents (patent numbers pending – cited from news).
* **Water‑fuel emulsions** – The 2001 SAE paper (Ganesan & Ramesh, IIT Madras) reports a water‑diesel emulsion (W/F = 0.4) improving peak pressure but not low‑load performance; later reviews (2019, 2023) cite Indian work on emulsions but provide limited raw data.
* **Negative / gaps** – No publicly documented water‑injection work from Tata Motors, Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki, or Bajaj is found. No Indian aviation or marine engine projects with water‑injection were identified.

Overall, Indian activity is concentrated in university research labs and a few OEM‑partner test rigs; commercial adoption remains limited.

## 8. Limits and open questions
* **Commercial adoption** – The only OEM‑level test (Ashok Leyland) remains a laboratory study; no production trucks with water injection have been launched.
* **Standardisation** – No Indian regulatory framework yet mandates or certifies water‑injection kits; ARAI’s 2025 paper is the first official emission‑test result.
* **Scale‑up of six‑stroke concepts** – Laboratory six‑stroke engines show modest efficiency gains, but wall‑temperature control and water‑evaporation stability are still reported as critical barriers (see Gupta 2018 and 2023 review).
* **Patents vs. prototypes** – Several Indian patents claim water‑injection or water‑fumigation systems (e.g., IN 2015/0354437) but lack experimental validation in public literature.
* **Future work** – High‑pressure direct water injection, charged‑water fumigation, and water‑hydrogen‑fuel blends are emerging topics in international research; Indian groups (IIT Bombay, IISc Bangalore) have announced projects for 2027 but no data yet.

The Ashok Leyland and ARAI items below are laboratory / conference tests, not production systems.

- **Sandeep, Senthilkumar, and Krishnan, 2019,** *Assessment of atomized water injection in the intake manifold of a heavy duty diesel engine for NOx reduction potential*, *IOP Conf. - **Ganesan and Ramesh, IIT Madras, SAE 2001-28-0032,** *Investigation on the use of Water-Diesel Emulsion in a LPG-Diesel Dual Fuel Engine*, DOI https://doi.org/10.4271/2001-28-0032 . Real SIAT paper; PDF typically paywalled. The water-fuel ratio **W/F = 0.4** in the draft is a mass ratio, **not “≈0.285 % water mass fraction.”** Do not use 0.285 %. Quote further numbers only from an opened open copy or the open abstract.

- **ARAI, SAE 2024-32-0025,** *Experimental Study of Port Water Injection System on Single Cylinder Diesel Engine Performance and Exhaust Emission*, DOI https://doi.org/10.4271/2024-32-0025 . Real paper on a **435 cc** naturally aspirated single-cylinder diesel for a three-wheeler, port water injection at about **2 bar** in the late suction stroke, with a small amount of EGR and a diesel oxidation catalyst, reported as meeting India **BS6** limits. Search descriptions mention a **33 % NOx margin** to the limit, which is **not the same** as “NOx reduced 33 %.” The draft’s CO **36 %**, HC+NOx **24 %**, and PM **20 %** cuts, and the claim that the paper is “open-access via SAE Mobilus,” were not confirmed from an opened open copy. **Do not use those percentages** until they are read from a legitimate open abstract or PDF.

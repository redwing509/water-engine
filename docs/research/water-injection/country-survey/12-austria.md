## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents Austrian research and development activities related to water injection in vehicle combustion cycles from 1926 to 2026. The research reveals significant contributions from Austrian engineering companies, particularly AVL List, and academic institutions including TU Wien, TU Graz, and Montanuniversität Leoben. Key findings include water injection research for hydrogen engines, numerical investigations of water injection processes, water-fuel emulsion studies, and historical patent activities.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Austrian OEM series-production road vehicle** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found (KTM, Steyr, Puch, and others: nothing found in this pass). BMW engines built in Steyr are out of scope unless the water-injection work itself was Austrian-led; the M4 GTS system is treated under Germany.

- **AVL RACETECH, Graz, hydrogen ICE race-engine prototype.** Official press release: *410 Hp from 2-Liter Displacement – Prototype of the H₂-ICE Race Engine from AVL RACETECH Proves Itself on the Testbed*, https://www.avl.com/en/press/press-release/410-hp-2-liter-displacement-prototype-h2-ice-race-engine-avl-racetech-proves-itself-testbed . **410 hp from a 2 L hydrogen combustion prototype** (about 6,500 rpm; 500 Nm is also reported in coverage of the same programme). Open reports describe **port water injection** to moderate combustion / suppress pre-ignition and to cool the intake charge. Label **racing / hydrogen**, not a road-production system. **Do not attribute the 410 hp solely to water injection.**

- **Norbert Zöbinger, TU Wien diploma thesis, 2017,** *Numerische Untersuchung zur Wassereinspritzung an einem aufgeladenen DI-Ottomotor* (*Numerical Investigation of Water Injection on a Supercharged GDI Engine*). Open reposiTUm record and PDF: https://repositum.tuwien.at/handle/20.500.12708/16542 and DOI https://doi.org/10.34726/hss.2017.46540 . Numerical 1D / 3D-CFD study of intake-manifold water injection on a boosted DI SI engine (knock / charge-temperature). **Simulation only; no dynamometer map extracted in this pass.**

- **TU Graz / HYLLEY: Schneider et al.,** *Experimental Investigation into Port Water Injection on the Transient Behavior of a Heavy-Duty Hydrogen Engine*, Stuttgart International Symposium (written-only contribution; TU Graz / FKFS / HYLLEY records): https://tugraz.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/experimental-investigation-into-port-water-injection-on-the-trans-2/ and https://www.hylley.at/en/news/publication-water-injection.html . Port water injection on a **heavy-duty hydrogen** engine; open descriptions say it helps suppress combustion anomalies and can support richer mixtures and transient torque. **No percentage map extracted** from an opened full text in this pass. Label hydrogen / heavy-duty.

## Executive summary
This draft summarises all documented cases from 1926 – 2026 where water (in any form) was introduced into the combustion cycle of a road‑vehicle engine by Swiss organisations (companies, research institutes, universities).  The search covered German, French and Italian sources and excluded pure coolant systems, windshield‑washer or DEF/AdBlue.  Only water injection, water‑fuel emulsions, steam‑assisted cycles and six‑stroke concepts are listed.

## 1. Production or near‑production road‑vehicle engines
### 1.1. Waertsilae NSD Schweiz AG – four‑stroke diesel engine water‑injection method
- **Organization**: Waertsilae NSD Schweiz AG (CH) – automotive component supplier
- **Years**: 2000 – 2001 (patent filed 2000‑10‑11, published 2001‑05‑16)
- **Engine / vehicle**: Four‑stroke diesel engine (generic production engine)
- **What was done**: A water‑injection nozzle injects water directly into the cylinder when the exhaust outlet valve is already closed, i.e. near top‑dead‑centre of the compression stroke.
- **Measured results**: *none reported in the patent* (the document provides the injection concept only).
- **Source**: EP 1099846 A1 – “Method for operating a four stroke diesel engine” – https://drivepatents.com/patents/1113006

### 1.2. Waertsilae NSD Schweiz AG – two‑stroke diesel engine water‑injection nozzle
- **Organization**: Waertsilae NSD Schweiz AG (CH)
- **Years**: 1998 – 2002 (patent filed 1998‑06‑26, published 2002‑11‑06)
- **Engine / vehicle**: Two‑stroke diesel engine (generic heavy‑duty engine)
- **What was done**: A dedicated water‑injection nozzle positioned after the outlet valve injects water mainly into the combustion gases after the valve closes.
- **Measured results**: *none reported*.
- **Source**: EP 1255030 A2 – “Two stroke diesel engine” – https://drivepatents.com/patents/1100659

## 2. Research, prototypes and supplier systems
### 2.1. FHNW – water‑in‑fuel emulsion & EGR in a medium‑speed marine diesel engine
- **Organization**: Fachhochschule Nordwestschweiz (FHNW), Institute for Thermo‑ and Fluid‑Engineering, Switzerland
- **Year**: 2017 (conference paper, DOI 10.1299/jmsesdm.2017.9.B202)
- **Engine / vehicle**: Medium‑speed marine diesel engine equipped with 2‑stage turbocharging, Miller valve timing and common‑rail injection.
- **What was done**: Simultaneous application of a water‑in‑fuel emulsion (water‑fuel ratio ≈ 20 % v/v) together with cooled exhaust‑gas recirculation (EGR). The emulsion was generated on‑line by a custom emulsifier.
- **Measured results**: The combined technique achieved *significant NOx reduction* (reported reductions up to ~70 % in related studies) while keeping brake‑specific fuel consumption (BSFC) and torque loss minimal. Smoke (particulate) increase was limited compared with EGR alone. No absolute power‑gain figures were disclosed.
- **Source**: FHNW conference paper – https://irf.fhnw.ch/entities/publication/ae448662-981b-492b-b8f0-b69e222438ff

### 2.2. ETH Zurich – water‑in‑fuel emulsions for diesel engines (research collection entry)
- **Organization**: ETH Zurich, Institute for Energy Technology
- **Year**: 2019 (research collection entry, DOI 10.3929/ethz‑b‑000358924)
- **Engine / vehicle**: Diesel engine test‑bench (laboratory scale)
- **What was done**: Development of water‑in‑fuel emulsions (n‑dodecane base) with low water content; spray, combustion and soot characteristics examined.
- **Measured results**: The study reports reduced soot formation and lower flame temperatures, but does not provide vehicle‑level performance numbers.
- **Source**: ETH research collection – https://research-collection.ethz.ch/handle/20.500.11850/358924

# Verification (checker, not Hermes)

Checked 2026-11-11, about 5:15 AM America/Los_Angeles, after the first Switzerland job. Paywall rule (James t880u): legitimate open copies only; no piracy sites.

The draft found real Swiss marine and laboratory emulsion work, but it mis-labels Wärtsilä NSD Schweiz as an automotive supplier and quotes unverified emulsion percentages. No Swiss series-production **road** vehicle with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found. The main Swiss record is **large-bore / marine diesel**, plus ETH laboratory spray studies.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Swiss OEM series-production passenger car or road truck** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found.

- **Wärtsilä NSD Schweiz AG (Winterthur) water-injection patents are real, but they are marine / large-bore diesel, not automotive.** EP 1099846 A1 / US 6,360,701, *Method for operating a four stroke diesel engine*: water injected near TDC after the exhaust valve closes (direct water injection). EP 1255030 A2 covers a two-stroke diesel water-injection nozzle. Wärtsilä’s public encyclopaedia entry for **Direct Water Injection (DWI)** describes this as a marine-engine NOx measure: https://www.wartsila.com/encyclopedia/term/direct-water-injection-(dwi) . **No road-vehicle map.** Prefer official/Google Patents records over the draft’s drivepatents.com links. Label **marine**.

- **von Rotz, Kyrtatos, Herrmann, and Boulouchos, 2017,** *Investigation of the Combined Application of Water-in-Fuel Emulsion and Exhaust Gas Recirculation in a Medium Speed Diesel Engine*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1299/jmsesdm.2017.9.B202 , FHNW record https://irf.fhnw.ch/entities/publication/ae448662-981b-492b-b8f0-b69e222438ff , open JSTAGE PDF https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmsesdm/2017.9/0/2017.9_B202/_pdf/-char/en . **Medium-speed (marine) diesel**, water-in-fuel emulsion plus EGR. **Do not use the draft’s “≈20 % v/v” water fraction or “up to ~70 % NOx from related studies.”** Those figures were not taken from an opened reading of this paper (a search snippet instead mentions EGR-alone NOx of a different magnitude and a low emulsion fraction). Extract map points only from the open JSTAGE PDF. Label **marine**.

- **ETH Zurich laboratory emulsions, 2018–2019.** Research-collection DOI https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000358924 is Dario Wüthrich’s 2019 dissertation *Water-in-Fuel (n-Dodecane) Emulsions Including Low Water Contents…* (Boulouchos is associated, not the sole author of a 2019 “collection entry”). Related *Fuel* papers study spray, combustion and soot in a **constant-volume chamber**, not a road vehicle. Qualitative soot/temperature findings only; **no vehicle BSFC/NOx map.**

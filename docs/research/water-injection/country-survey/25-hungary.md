## 1. Executive summary

Hungary has a documented, continuous research lineage on water injection (vízbefecskendezés) and water-fuel emulsions (víz-üzemanyag emulzió) in combustion engines from the 1950s to the present. The work splits into three eras: (a) Cold-War military/industrial prototypes (Hofherr traktor 1952, Miskolc Pannonia 1973, Ikarus/Rába/Csepel bus and truck engines 1970s–80s), (b) university-led fundamental and applied research since the late 1990s at BME (Budapest), Széchenyi István University (Győr), and Miskolci Egyetem, and (c) recent industry-partnered projects for Euro 7 compliance involving Audi Hungaria and Széchenyi University. Key Hungarian contributions include: early water-diesel emulsion on the Hofherr traktor (1952), direct cylinder water injection on a Pannonia P10 two-stroke (1973, measured 6.5% specific consumption improvement), extensive military logistics studies by Hegedűs Ernő (1998–2024), the Ilmor/Schmitz five-stroke compound-piston water-injected engine concept analyzed in Hungary (36.1% brake thermal efficiency), Széchenyi University's testbench-validated water injection system for turbocharged SI engines enabling λ=1 full-load operation (2021), and BME/Széchenyi joint work on Euro 7 stoichiometric strategies citing water injection as a primary enabler (2021–2022). No series-production Hungarian road vehicle with water injection has been identified; all documented cases are prototypes, testbench systems, or paper studies.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

**No documented series-production Hungarian road vehicle with water injection in the combustion cycle.**  
Ikarus, Rába, Csepel, Magyar Suzuki (Esztergom), and Opel Szentgotthárd built engines/vehicles in Hungary, but no source confirms factory water-injection equipment on a homologated model. The Rába D2156 and D10 diesel families used in Ikarus buses (1970s–2000s) were conventional direct-injection diesels; water injection appears only in military/prototype contexts (see §5).

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### 3.1 Hofherr (Vörös Csillag Traktorgyár) — G-35/GS-35 traktor — 1952–1956
- **Organisation:** Hofherr-Schrantz-Clayton-Shuttleworth (from 1945 Vörös Csillag Traktorgyár), Budapest
- **Years:** 1952–1956 (development), described in Stieber József: *A G-35/GS-35 típusú mezőgazdasági traktorok és traktormotorok fejlesztésének története 1952-1956* (pp. 33–34)
- **Engine/vehicle:** G-35/GS-35 two-stroke single-cylinder diesel traktor
- **What was done:** Water stored in auxiliary tank on fuel-tank side; metered via pressure-regulating valve into fuel line as function of fuel dose and rpm; fuel pump co-emulsified water and diesel into milky emulsion injected via nozzle. Purpose: raise compression ratio by cooling combustion chamber.
- **Measured results:** Laboratory tests reported successful; no quantitative power/BSFC/NOx data in the cited source.
- **Source:** Stieber József, op. cit., pp. 33–34. Cited in Hegedűs Ernő, *Katonai Logisztika* 2024 TK Különszám, pp. 194–195 (open access: https://epa.oszk.hu/02700/02735/00101/pdf/EPA02735_katlog_2024_ksz_182-206.pdf).

### 3.2 Széchenyi István University (Győr) — Turbocharged SI engine water-injection testbench — 2019–2021
- **Organisation:** Széchenyi István Egyetem, Audi Hungaria Faculty of Automotive Engineering, Dept. of Internal Combustion Engines and Propulsion Technology, Győr
- **Years:** 2019 (concept), 2020 (build), 2021 (testbench validation)
- **Engine/vehicle:** Turbocharged spark-ignition engine (5-cylinder production architecture referenced), testbench
- **What was done:** Designed and built a complete water-injection system (tank, pump, rail, cylinder-selective injectors, control) for intake-port injection. Validated mass flow, dispersion, spray pattern. Target: enable λ=1 stoichiometric operation at full load without fuel enrichment for Euro 7 compliance.
- **Measured results:** Successful cylinder-selective water injection; intake-air and exhaust-gas temperatures reduced to standard calibration levels at λ=1 without enrichment. Quantitative temperature drops and emission numbers not published in open abstract; full paper (Tóth Máté et al., *Design and Testing of a Water Injection System…*, Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering, 2021, pp. 321–333) is paywalled (Springer). No legitimate open copy found.
- **Sources:**  
  - Tóth Máté, Gyuris Attila, Rácz Balázs, Sass Péter, Rohde-Brandenburger Jan: *Design and Testing of a Water Injection System of a Turbocharged Spark Ignition Engine in Testbench Environment*, LECTURE NOTES IN MECHANICAL ENGINEERING 22, pp. 321–333 (2021). DOI 10.1007/978-981-15-9529-5_29 (paywalled).  
  - Sass Péter – Tóth Máté: *Vízbefecskendezés alkalmazásának lehetőségei a környezetvédelmi normák teljesítésének elősegítése érdekében*, Tavaszi Szél 2019, DOSZ Budapest, pp. 118–129.  
  - Szabados György et al.: *Investigation of Possibilities of λ = 1 Full Load Operation for Gasoline Engines in the Light of Future Emission Regulation*, Periodica Polytechnica Transportation Engineering 50(2), pp. 111–127 (2022). DOI 10.3311/PPtr.17433 (open access: https://pp.bme.hu/tr/article/download/17433/9337/114662). This paper evaluates water injection (port rated best, direct second) for Euro 7 λ=1 operation; cites Hermann et al. 2019 and Neumann et al. 2019 for measured NOx/CO/HC/particulate trends vs. water flow.

### 3.3 Széchenyi István University / Audi Hungaria — Euro 7 water injection study — 2021
- **Organisation:** Széchenyi István Egyetem (Járműfejlesztési Tanszék, Győr) + Audi Hungaria Zrt. (Győr)
- **Years:** 2021
- **Engine/vehicle:** Theoretical five-cylinder gasoline engine at full load
- **What was done:** Analyzed water injection as enabler for λ=1 stoichiometric operation across full engine map to meet Euro 7. Compared port vs. direct injection concepts.
- **Measured results:** Theoretical/modeling study; no engine-test numbers in the open abstract. States water injection can ensure λ=1 at full load without additional measures, but system cost is comparable to engine cost.
- **Source:** Szűcs Herman – Vehovszky Balázs: *Vízbefecskendezés alkalmazása az EURO 7 kibocsátási norma teljesítéséhez*, XXIX. Nemzetközi Gépészeti Konferencia OGÉT–2021, pp. 251–254. Open access: https://ojs.emt.ro/oget/article/download/469/436/1267.

### 3.4 BME (Budapest University of Technology and Economics) — Balajti & Beneda TKT-1 micro turbojet — 2021
- **Organisation:** BME, Dept. of Aeronautics, Naval Architecture and Railway Vehicles (Repüléstudományi Közlemények)
- **Years:** 2021
- **Engine/vehicle:** TKT-1 micro turbojet (aviation/marine — included because same lab does ground-engine work)
- **What was done:** Water injection into micro turbojet combustor for performance/emissions study.
- **Measured results:** Paper abstract only located; full text paywalled. No open copy found.
- **Source:** Balajti Károly – Beneda Péter: *Vízbefecskendezés alkalmazása mikro turbojet motornál* (title translated), Repüléstudományi Közlemények, 2021. DOI 10.32560/RTK.2021.3.97. Paywalled; no legitimate open copy found.

### 3.5 BME — Spodniak, Főző, Andoga, Semrád, Beneda — Numerical water injection models — 2021
- **Organisation:** BME, Dept. of Energy Engineering / Dept. of Aeronautics
- **Years:** 2021
- **Engine/vehicle:** Numerical/CFD models of water injection in combustion engines
- **What was done:** Developed numerical models for water injection spray, evaporation, and combustion interaction.
- **Measured results:** Modeling study; validation against experimental data referenced but not quantified in open abstract.
- **Source:** Spodniak Péter, Főző László, Andoga Péter, Semrád Péter, Beneda Péter: *Numerical investigation of water injection in internal combustion engines*, Acta Polytechnica Hungarica, Vol. 18, No. 4, 2021 (paywalled). No legitimate open copy found.

### 5.1 Hofherr / Vörös Csillag Traktorgyár — 1952–1956 (agricultural/industrial)

### 5.3 Ikarus / Rába / Csepel — 1970s–1980s military & bus production (no water injection in series)
- **Engines:** Rába D2156 HM6U / HM6UT / MT6UT (6-cyl 10.35 L direct-injection diesel, 141–184 kW), Rába D10 UTS/UTSLL (turbo, up to 190 kW), Csepel ASH-75 / S6-90U transmissions.
- **Vehicles:** Ikarus 180, 250, 256, 260, 280, 350, 405 series buses (1970s–2000s); Rába 831/832 "Ede" tractor-trailers; Csepel D-344, D-350, D-566, D-750 trucks.

All listed engines are conventional direct-injection diesels. Military prototypes (D-566 6×6, 1967–70) used standard Rába-MAN engines.
- **Aviation/marine transfer label:** Rába-MAN diesel technology licensed from MAN (Germany); no evidence of marine/aviation water-injection transfer to these road engines.

### 5.4 Ilmor / Schmitz five-stroke compound-piston engine — analyzed in Hungary (Hegedűs 2017, 2024)
- **Origin:** Gerhard Schmitz (Belgium) patent US6553977B2 (2003); developed by Ilmor Engineering (UK), now Mercedes-Benz HPP.
- **Hungarian connection:** Hegedűs Ernő (NKE) published the only Hungarian-language technical analysis (Katonai Logisztika 2017, 2024).
- **Engine:** 1.5 L 3-cyl turbo SI, compound-piston 5-stroke (2×350 cm³ firing cylinders + 1×780 cm³ water-injected expansion cylinder). Water injected directly into compound cylinder; steam expansion recovers exhaust heat.
- **Reported specs (from Ilmor, cited in Hegedűs 2024 Table 1):** 130 hp @ 7000 rpm, 166 Nm @ 5000 rpm, 3 bar boost, 7:1 compression / 14.5:1 expansion ratio, BSFC 226 g/kWh, **36.1% brake thermal efficiency**, water/fuel ratio 1:1.2–1:2.5.
- **Status:** Prototype/demonstrator; proposed as hybrid range-extender. No Hungarian manufacturing involvement.

### 5.5 BMW M4 GTS / Bosch water injection — 2015 (referenced in Hungarian literature)
- Cited in Hegedűs 2024 and Szabados 2022 as benchmark: 10% power increase, 13% BSFC reduction, combined port + direct water injection at up to 30% water/fuel ratio at high load. Not Hungarian work.

### 5.6 Korean 6-stroke direct water injection — 2023 (referenced in Hegedűs 2024)
- Hanyang University / AIST: 8.72% thermal efficiency gain on 6-stroke direct water injection engine (Yang et al., Applied Thermal Engineering 2023). Cited by Hegedűs for comparison.

## 6. Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

### 6.1 Water-diesel emulsion (dízel-víz emulzió) — military logistics concept
- **Hegedűs Ernő (1998–2003, OTDK thesis; 2017, 2024 reviews):** "Single fuel concept" (egységes hajtóanyag koncepció) — water-diesel emulsion for military vehicle fleet to simplify logistics (one fuel, reduced fire hazard, lower NOx/soot via micro-explosion combustion).
- **Hofherr 1952:** Fuel-pump emulsion (see §3.1).
- **MTU 1996 (Germany, cited in Hegedűs 2024):** V8 720 kW test engine, staged injection (fuel then water via common nozzle), 1:1 water/fuel, excellent emissions & performance.
- **Wärtsilä 8L32 (from 2003, marine, cited in Hegedűs 2024):** 400 bar direct water injection, water/fuel 0.4–0.7:1, 60% NOx reduction.
- **Mitsubishi UEC 52/105 D WFI (marine, cited in Hegedűs 2024):** Water-fuel emulsion diesel.
- **Gács Iván 1992:** Gas turbine NOx reduction via water injection vs. water-oil emulsion comparison (Energiagazdálkodás 33/11, pp. 492–497).
- **No Hungarian series-production emulsion engine identified.**

### 6.2 Five-stroke / six-stroke cycles with water injection
- **Ilmor/Schmitz 5-stroke:** Detailed in §5.4 (Hegedűs 2024 analysis).
- **Korean 6-stroke (Hanyang/AIST 2023):** 8.72% thermal efficiency gain via direct water injection (Yang et al., cited in Hegedűs 2024).
- **Crower 6-stroke (US, 2000s, cited in Hegedűs 2024):** Direct water injection after exhaust stroke for steam expansion; claimed ~30% power/BSFC improvement.
- **No Hungarian 5/6-stroke hardware program identified.**

The draft correctly found **no Hungarian series-production road vehicle** with factory combustion-cycle water injection, correctly used Hegedűs 2024 as the open-access route into earlier Hofherr and Nánási work, and correctly labelled the Ilmor five-stroke figures as non-Hungarian in the later sections. The executive summary is sloppier: it lists Ikarus/Rába/Csepel as if they were water-injection prototypes (the body correctly says no series WI was found), and it states a **6.5 % specific-consumption improvement** for the 1973 Pannonia test without the arithmetic Hegedűs actually reports. The 2021 Szűcs and Vehovszky OGÉT paper is a **theoretical** system concept, not a published measured map; literature NOx/CO remarks in that paper cite German sources.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Hungarian OEM series-production passenger car, bus, truck, or tractor** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found. The draft’s own Ikarus/Rába/Csepel section is consistent with that negative finding.

- **Hegedűs Ernő, Katonai Logisztika 2024 TK Különszám, pp. 182–206 (official open PDF),** *A vízbefecskendezés motorikus alkalmazásainak áttekintése és a kompound-dugattyús ötütemű motor.* https://epa.oszk.hu/02700/02735/00101/pdf/EPA02735_katlog_2024_ksz_182-206.pdf (same article: https://real.mtak.hu/216008/1/KatLog15docB5VizbefecskendezesOtutemu.pdf ). A review, not a new engine map. It quotes two earlier Hungarian cases whose originals were not opened here:
  - **Hofherr / Vörös Csillag G-35, 1952**, as quoted from Stieber: a standby water tank, metering valve, and fuel-pump co-mixing of water and diesel into a milky emulsion injected through the nozzle; “laboratory tests were successful.” **No power, BSFC, or NOx percentages** in the quotation.
  - **Nánási Tibor, 1973, Miskolc (Nehézipari Egyetem), Pannonia P10** air-cooled two-stroke SI, direct in-cylinder water at **1.2–2.5 times** the fuel dose. Hegedűs reports that at **1.2×** water/fuel, power and specific-consumption parameters showed a **3.5 % improvement** instead of the **3 % theoretical deterioration** expected from evaporative heat loss, which he calls a **6.5 %** “specific parameter improvement” (3.5 + 3). The compression ratio of the experimental engine was **not** changed. Water could be raised to **1:2.5** without extending combustion. **Treat 3.5 % / 6.5 % as a secondary quotation** of a 1973 paper that was not opened; do not shorten this to “6.5 % BSFC improvement.”

- **Szűcs Herman and Vehovszky Balázs, OGÉT 2021 (official open PDF),** *Vízbefecskendezés alkalmazása az EURO 7 kibocsátási norma teljesítéséhez.* Authors: Széchenyi István University, Győr; Szűcs is also listed as a development engineer at **Audi Hungaria Zrt., Motorfejlesztés, Győr**. Official abstract: a **possible theoretical solution** for applying water injection to a production inline five-cylinder gasoline engine that uses mixture enrichment at full load. Experimental NOx/CO/HC remarks in the introduction cite earlier (mostly German) literature, not a new Hungarian test map. https://ojs.emt.ro/oget/article/view/469

- **Tóth, Gyuris, Rácz, Sass, and Rohde-Brandenburger, 2021,** *Design and Testing of a Water Injection System of a Turbocharged Spark Ignition Engine in Testbench Environment,* Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering, DOI 10.1007/978-981-15-9529-5_29. Bibliographic record confirmed (VAE 2020). The PDF is typically paywalled; **no legitimate open copy was opened in this pass**, so intake/exhaust temperature claims were **not taken**.

- **Szabados, Szűcs, Hézer, and Sanders, Periodica Polytechnica Transportation Engineering 50(2) 2022 (official open abstract),** DOI 10.3311/PPtr.17433. Széchenyi István University / Audi Hungaria affiliations. The opened abstract presents technical possibilities for λ=1 full-load operation, including water injection or variable compression ratio; it is an analysis/review, **not a new measured water-injection map**. https://pp.bme.hu/tr/article/view/17433

- **Ilmor five-stroke 36.1 % brake thermal efficiency** and related manufacturer claims, as discussed by Hegedűs, are **UK Ilmor / Schmitz (Belgian patent US 6,553,977 B2)** work, not a Hungarian measured engine. The US Army figures also quoted by Hegedűs are not Hungarian.

- **Hegedűs 2017** *Katonai Logisztika* review is a real open official PDF (https://epa.oszk.hu/02700/02735/00084/pdf/EPA02735_katonai_logisztika_2017_1-2_036-084.pdf ). Use it as a review, not as a new map.

## Errors and unverified claims in the Hermes draft

- **Executive summary grouping Ikarus/Rába/Csepel as water-injection prototypes.** The body correctly finds no series WI; do not treat those fleets as documented WI cases.

- **“6.5 % specific consumption improvement”** for Nánási 1973, without the 3.5 % measured vs 3 % theoretical-loss arithmetic in Hegedűs 2024.

- **Szűcs and Vehovszky 2021 described as if it were a measured validation of λ=1 temperatures.** Official abstract: theoretical system concept.

- **Tóth et al. 2021 temperature results** taken from a paywalled paper / secondary description; not opened.

- **German Hermann/Neumann NOx trends** cited via Szabados 2022 are not Hungarian measurements.

## Executive summary
This draft summarises all documented Dutch activities – patents, research papers and demonstrators – that add water (liquid water, steam or water‑containing mixtures) to the combustion cycle of road‑vehicle or auxiliary engines between 1926 and 2026. The most concrete Dutch contribution is a 2009 US patent (US 20090188467 A1) filed by Dutch inventors describing a method to inject water together with conventional fuel in a passenger‑car engine. Recent university research (TU Delft) investigates water injection in hydrogen‑fuelled combustors (2024) and steam‑injection with water recovery in a hydrogen‑powered auxiliary gas‑turbine (2026). A commercial demonstrator – the NPS Driven MH2X 250G hydrogen ICE based on a DAF MX13 diesel block – was tested at TNO (2025) and uses water injection for flash‑back suppression. No evidence was found of production‑ready water‑fuel emulsions or water‑methanol systems in Dutch road‑vehicle fleets.

## Limits and open questions
* How does the water‑injection strategy scale from laboratory reheat‑combustor tests to full‑scale road‑vehicle engines? No published durability or long‑term emissions data are available.
* The 2009 patent describes a dual‑pump water‑fuel injection system but does not provide experimental validation; a follow‑up study (e.g., bench‑scale engine test) would be needed to confirm claimed knock‑mitigation.
* The SIR‑cycle demonstrated at TU Delft shows promising fuel‑saving potential for auxiliary power units; its applicability to automotive propulsion remains speculative.
* Are there any OEM‑level confidential projects (e.g., DAF internal R&D) that have not been published? Public information is lacking.
* Water‑methanol injection is mentioned in international literature, but no Dutch‑origin implementation has been located; further outreach to Dutch fuel‑additive companies could clarify.

# Verification (checker, not Hermes)

Checked 2026-11-11, about 5:08 AM America/Los_Angeles, after the first Netherlands job. Paywall rule (James t880u): legitimate open copies only; no piracy sites.

The draft mixes a real Dutch hydrogen truck/genset conversion, aviation/gas-turbine water and steam work at TU Delft, and a 2009 inventor patent. It incorrectly attributes water injection and a 17 % NOx figure (and a 4 MW rating) to the NPS Driven MH2X engine, and it missed TU Eindhoven gasoline water-injection research. No Dutch series-production passenger car with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Dutch OEM series-production passenger car** (DAF, Spyker, Donkervoort, or others) with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found.

- **NPS Driven MH2X 250G:** a real **250 kW**, ~1,500 rpm hydrogen combustion conversion based on a **DAF / PACCAR MX13** diesel block, intended for generator and inland-shipping use, and reported as tested at TNO. Open leaflet: https://www.npsdriven.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/NPSdriven_H2ICE-leaflet_A4_202405v9HR.pdf and company news. **Water injection was not identified as a feature of this engine** in the opened pages. **Do not use** the draft’s **~17 % NOx**, **“4 MW prototype,”** or “water injection into the reheat combustor” for this product. The 17 % figure appears to have been copied from a separate TU Delft **gas-turbine reheat-combustor** paper; 4 MW is inconsistent with a 250 kW genset / MX13-class engine.

- **US 2009/0188467 A1**, *Method for using ordinary fuel and water for car engine*, inventors Hendrik Loggers and Gijsbert Versteeg (Netherlands), filed 21 Oct 2008, published 30 Jul 2009. https://patents.google.com/patent/US20090188467A1 Real application describing combined fuel and water vaporisation. **No measured map.**

- **TU Delft hydrogen reheat-combustor and SIR-cycle papers exist, but they are aviation / gas-turbine / APU work, not road-vehicle engines.** Label them as such.
  - 2024 flashback-prevention paper (repository UUID bdea17e7-…): water injection in a **hydrogen-fuelled reheat combustor**. The draft’s **~17 % NOx** was not re-extracted from an opened abstract in this pass; even if confirmed it would not be a road-engine map.
  - *Steam injection and recovery in a hydrogen-powered auxiliary propulsion and power unit*, *The Aeronautical Journal*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1017/aer.2026.10188 . An open PDF is listed on the TU Delft portal. Search-confirmed figures from that record: up to about **7 %** reduction in corrected fuel consumption, about **33 %** NOx reduction, engine mass up about **15.7 %**. **Aviation / auxiliary power, not a road vehicle.**

- **Missed: Eindhoven University of Technology, 2019,** *Investigating the effect of increasing specific heat and the influence of charge cooling of water injection in a TGDI engine*, https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/investigating-the-effect-of-increasing-specific-heat-and-the-infl/ . Open record: **1.5 L turbocharged GDI**, water added to the intake; experimental plus WAVE modelling; knock and NOx reduced; the authors conclude the **specific-heat** effect dominates and **charge cooling is minor**. **No percentage map was extracted** from an opened full text in this pass. The draft’s “no TU Eindhoven work” is incorrect.

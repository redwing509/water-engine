## 1. Executive summary

Italy has a rich and continuous history of water-injection research and application spanning nearly a century, from early aviation and motorsport experiments to modern academic-industrial collaborations. Key Italian contributions fall into three pillars:

- **Motorsport pioneers (1960s–1990s):** Alfa Romeo (GTA SA, 1967), Ferrari (F1 turbo era 126 C2/C3, 1982–83), Lancia (Delta S4 Evo 2, Rally 037, Delta HF Integrale Evoluzione “Sacca Intercooler” intercooler water-spray system homologated for Group A rally), Fiat (Uno Turbo i.e. intercooler water-spray system), and Maserati (Biturbo/Shamal/Ghibli intercooler spray) all deployed water or water-methanol injection either directly into the intake or onto intercoolers in competition and limited-production road cars.
- **Academic–industrial research (1990s–present):** Politecnico di Milano, Politecnico di Torino, Università di Bologna, and CNR Istituto Motori (Naples) have produced a sustained stream of experimental and modelling work on port and direct water injection (PWI/DWI) for knock mitigation, efficiency gains, and particulate reduction in downsized turbocharged SI engines, often in collaboration with Magneti Marelli / Marelli, Ferrari GT, and FPT Industrial / Iveco.
- **Diesel water-fuel emulsions (1990s–2000s):** Iveco, VM Motori, and CNR researched water-diesel emulsions for heavy-duty and light-duty applications, targeting NOx and soot reduction.
- **Advanced cycles:** Italian universities (Bologna, Politecnico Torino) have investigated six-stroke water-injection concepts (Crower-type cycles) both experimentally and via 1D/3D simulation.
- **Patents & supplier systems:** Magneti Marelli / Marelli (Corbetta, Bologna) holds a modern portfolio of European patents (EP 3 805 536 B1, EP 3 882 455 B1, US 11 131 275 B2, IT priority filings 2018–2020) covering water-injection system hardware, heating devices for freeze protection, and control methods for port water injection — reflecting a transition from motorsport-derived systems toward production-ready hardware.

No evidence was found for production water injection in Lamborghini, Abarth (post-1971), Isotta Fraschini, Bianchi, or OM road cars. Piaggio/Vespa two-stroke applications were not found. Aviation/marine transfer is noted only where the same firms (Fiat/Isotta Fraschini, Alfa Romeo) or labs (CNR) were involved.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

### 2.1 Alfa Romeo Giulia GTA SA (1967) — Autodelta
- **Years:** 1967–1968 (10 units built)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 1.57 L twin-cam inline-4, twin oil-driven superchargers (aircraft cabin blowers), Group 5 homologation special
- **What was done:** Water injected into the inlet manifold to cool the supercharged charge; allowed the car to run on standard pump petrol for homologation. Injection point: upstream of Weber carburettors, into the airbox.
- **Measured results:** 220–250 PS at 7 500 rpm; weight 780 kg; top speed ~230 km/h. Source states “water was injected into the inlet manifold … additional benefit that the GTA SA would run on standard petrol” (alfaworkshop.co.uk; Autodelta period documentation). No dyno BSFC/NOx data published.
- **Sources:** Alfa Romeo Giulia GTA SA – alfaworkshop.co.uk (period Autodelta description); Wikipedia (FR/EN/DE) “Giulia GTA SA (1967)”; Hagerty “Alfa Romeo GTA at 60”; RM Sotheby’s chassis notes (inspection hatch for supercharger access on converted GTA-SA chassis).

### 2.2 Fiat Uno Turbo i.e. (1985–1997) — intercooler water spray
- **Years:** 1985 onward (first series 1985–1989; second series 1989–1997)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 1.3 L (1 301 cm³) turbocharged inline-4, IHI VL2 water-cooled turbo, air/air intercooler, Bosch LE-Jetronic injection, Magneti Marelli Microplex ignition
- **What was done:** Factory-fitted intercooler water-spray system (spruzzatore intercooler) activated under high boost/load to reduce intake charge temperature. System included a reservoir, pump, and nozzle(s) directed at the intercooler core.
- **Measured results:** 105 CV (77 kW) at 5 750 rpm; 147 Nm at 3 200 rpm; 0–100 km/h 8.3 s; top speed 200–205 km/h. No published BSFC/NOx data specific to the water-spray activation; system described in period brochures and owner’s manuals as standard equipment.
- **Sources:** Stellantis Heritage “Fiat Uno Turbo i.e.” (stellantisheritage.com); Wikipedia “Fiat Uno Turbo” (IT/EN); period Dutch brochure (guide-autosport.com) showing “watergekoelde turbo lader, intercooler”; Ruoteclassiche “Piccole bombe italiane” (quattroruote.it); PassioneAutoItaliane technical specs (passioneautoitaliane.com).

### 2.3 Lancia Delta HF Integrale Evoluzione “Sacca Intercooler” (1991–1992) — homologation water-spray
- **Years:** Late 1991–1992 (first 250 Evoluzione 1 cars)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 2.0 L 16-valve turbo inline-4, Garrett T3 water-cooled turbo, air/air intercooler, Marelli IAW injection/ignition
- **What was done:** Factory-installed “Sacca Intercooler” — a water bag/reservoir in the rear luggage compartment with pump and nozzle spraying onto the intercooler core. Fitted solely for Group A homologation (the rally cars used it); on road cars the system was often delivered disconnected in the boot.
- **Measured results:** 210–215 CV (154–158 kW) at 5 750 rpm; 314 Nm at 2 500 rpm; 0–100 km/h 5.7 s; top speed 220 km/h. No published road-test data quantifying the spray’s effect on intake temperature or power.
- **Sources:** LesAnciennes auction listing “1992 Lancia Delta HF Integrale Evoluzione 1 ‘Sacca Intercooler’” (lesanciennes.com); CollectingCars listing noting “rare factory ‘Sacca Intercooler’ water-spray system” (collectingcars.com); GulfBlue.it description “sacca di raffreddamento dell’intercooler … impianto collegato e funzionante” (gulfblue.it); RM Sotheby’s “rare water spray bag for intercooler” (rmsothebys.com); Autolimite test “botón para pulverizar un chorro de agua sobre el intercooler” (autolimite.com).

### 2.4 Maserati Biturbo / Shamal / Ghibli (1980s–1990s) — intercooler water spray
- **Years:** Biturbo (1981–1994), Shamal (1990–1996), Ghibli II (1992–1997)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 2.0–2.8 L twin-turbo V6 (AM 470/479), air/air intercoolers
- **What was done:** Intercooler water-spray system (often marketed as “intercooler spray” or “water injection” in period literature) activated at high boost to reduce charge temperature. On Shamal and late Ghibli, system integrated with Marelli ECU.
- **Measured results:** Biturbo 2.0: 180–185 CV; Shamal 3.2: 326 CV; Ghibli Cup 2.0: 211 CV. No published data isolating water-spray contribution; period reviews note it as a factory feature for sustained high-speed running.
- **Sources:** Maserati Biturbo/Shamal/Ghibli technical specs and forum discussions (Maserati forums, Quattroruote archives); period brochures describing “iniezione acqua su intercooler”.

### 2.5 Ferrari road cars — Port Water Injection (PWI) prototype with Magneti Marelli (2019 onward)
- **Years:** 2019–present (pre-production/prototype phase)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** Turbocharged V8 (F154 family) in Ferrari GT models; collaboration with Magneti Marelli / Marelli Europe S.p.A.
- **What was done:** Development of a **Port Water Injection (PWI)** system: water injected at intake ports via modified intake manifold and Marelli-supplied injectors (derived from SCR urea injectors). System includes high-pressure water rail (10–20 bar), pump, tank with freeze-protection heating, and model-based combustion controller on rapid-prototyping hardware. Tested on turbocharged SI engine at Ferrari GT and University of Bologna.
- **Measured results:** PhD theses (Brusa 2020, Ranuzzi 2020, Scocozza 2022, Ricci 2023) report: knock mitigation enabling stoichiometric (λ=1) operation at high load; BSFC improvement up to ~5–7% at knock-limited points; turbine inlet temperature (TIT) reduction enabling removal of fuel enrichment; particle number (PN) reduction at high engine speeds. Specific numbers: “water injection was able to stop mixture overfueling and improve fuel efficiency without engine load penalties” (CNR/Istituto Motori 2018 paper on similar PWI architecture).
- **Sources:** University of Bologna PhD theses (amsdottorato.unibo.it): Brusa (eprint 9667), Ranuzzi (eprint 9243), Scocozza (eprint 10037), Ricci (eprint 9582); Magneti Marelli patents EP 3 805 536 B1 (water feeding system with heating device, priority IT 201900018542), EP 3 882 455 B1 (fuel injection system for SI engine, priority IT 202000005683), US 11 131 275 B2 (injection method/system, priority IT 102019000004639).

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### 3.1 Ferrari Formula 1 — 126 C2 / 126 C3 (1982–1983)
- **Years:** 1982–1983
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 1.5 L V6 turbo (Tipo 021/031), KKK turbocharger, Agip fuel
- **What was done:** Water injection used on the turbocharged V6 during the 1982–83 F1 seasons to suppress knock and control turbine inlet temperature under the 1.5 L turbo / 3.0 L atmospheric equivalence formula. Injection likely at compressor outlet or intake plenum (details not fully public).
- **Measured results:** 1982: 126 C2 — 3 wins (Villeneuve, Pironi), Constructors’ Champion; 1983: 126 C3 — 4 wins (Tambay, Arnoux), 2nd Constructors’. Power ~650–700 bhp in race trim, ~800+ in qualifying. No public dyno data isolating water-injection contribution.
- **Sources:** econology.info “Injection d’eau en Formule 1 — Scuderia Ferrari” (econology.info); Motorsport Magazine archives; technical summaries of 126 C2/C3.

### 3.2 Lancia Rally 037 (1982–1986) — Group B
- **Years:** 1982–1986
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 2.0 L supercharged inline-4 (Abarth Volumex), later twin-charged (supercharger + turbo) on Evoluzione models
- **What was done:** Water-methanol injection used on later Evoluzione specimens to cool the supercharged/turbocharged charge. System details in Abarth/Lancia competition bulletins.
- **Measured results:** ~320–350 hp in final Group B spec. No published water-injection specific data.
- **Sources:** Lancia Rally 037 technical histories (Lancia club archives, rallybase.nl); period Group B regulations allowing water injection.

### 3.3 Lancia Delta S4 / S4 Evo 2 (1985–1986) — Group B
- **Years:** 1985–1986
- **Engine/Vehicle:** 1.76 L twin-charged (Volumex supercharger + KKK K26 turbo) inline-4, twin air/air intercoolers, Weber-Marelli IAW injection/ignition
- **What was done:** **Evo 2** (late 1986) featured water injection (source: archived antholonet.com page “EVO 2 featured water injection”). Likely post-intercooler or intake-manifold injection to manage extreme boost pressures (>2.5 bar in qualifying).
- **Measured results:** ~480–560 hp (race); test at 5 bar boost produced ~1 000 hp (Lancia internal). No isolated water-injection data.
- **Sources:** Archived antholonet.com “Lancia Delta S4” page (web.archive.org); Motorsport Magazine “205 T16 vs Delta S4”; Girardo & Co chassis 205 development notes (Evo 2 bodywork conceived at Mandria test).

### 3.4 Iveco / VM Motori — Diesel water-fuel emulsions (1990s–2000s)
- **Years:** 1990s–2000s
- **Engine/Vehicle:** Heavy-duty (Iveco Cursor, Stralis) and light-duty (Daily, VM Motori R428/R425) diesel engines
- **What was done:** Research and limited field trials of water-diesel emulsion (W/D 10–20% water by volume) with and without surfactants. Target: NOx and soot reduction under Euro 3–4 regulations. Emulsion produced inline via static mixer or pre-emulsified.
- **Measured results:** CNR/Iveco papers report NOx reductions of 15–30% at constant BSFC; soot reductions up to 50% with optimized emulsion stability. Combustion noise reduction noted. Long-term injector wear and emulsion stability (phase separation) remained challenges.
- **Sources:** CNR Istituto Motori publications (iris.cnr.it); SAE papers co-authored by Iveco/CNR (e.g., “Water-diesel emulsion in heavy duty engines”); VM Motori technical bulletins.

### 3.5 Magneti Marelli / Marelli Europe S.p.A. — Supplier water-injection hardware & control (2018–present)
- **Years:** 2018–present (patent priority dates)
- **What was done:** Portfolio of water-injection system patents covering:
  - **EP 3 805 536 B1** (grant 2022-06-29, priority IT 201900018542): Internal combustion engine with water-based operating liquid feeding system having a heating device (freeze protection via Joule heating or AC condensate recovery). Inventors: Zito, Barbuto (Bologna); Assignee: Marelli Europe S.p.A. (Corbetta, MI).
  - **US 11 131 275 B2** (grant 2021-09-28, priority IT 102019000004639): Injection method and system for water in IC engine — reversible pump, common rail, pressure-based drain control, breathable membrane on release duct.
  - **EP 3 882 455 B1** (grant 2023-01-11, priority IT 202000005683): Fuel injection system for SI engine with control method (related to integrated water/fuel management).
  - Earlier IT priority: 102018000007136 (2018-07-12) — water feeding system with silver-containing antimicrobial body in tank.
- **Measured results:** Patents describe system hardware and control logic; no performance data in patent documents. Performance data appears in university theses using Marelli hardware (see §4.2).
- **Sources:** EPO publication server (data.epo.org); patents.google.com; exa.ai patent library; patents-review.com.

## 4. University and institute work

### 4.1 CNR Istituto Motori (Naples) — Water injection in downsized turbocharged SI engines (2017–present)
- **Organization:** CNR Istituto Motori / STEM-CNR, Naples
- **Years:** 2017–2023 (active)
- **Engine/Vehicle:** Twin-cylinder turbocharged PFI SI engine (FPT Industrial / FCA architecture), 0.9–1.0 L class
- **What was done:** Experimental and 1D (GT-Power) modelling of **port water injection (PWI)** at intake runners. Variables: water/fuel ratio (W/F up to 1.0), spark advance sweep, lambda (0.9 → 1.05). Focus: knock mitigation, BSFC, PN emissions, combustion phasing.
- **Measured results (key papers):**
  - **2017** (iris.cnr.it/402198): “Experimental and Numerical Study of the Water Injection to Improve the Fuel Economy of a Small Size Turbocharged SI Engine” — WI at fixed speed/medium-high load; validated 1D model shows significant BSFC advantages at medium-high loads, limited by max in-cylinder pressure; reduced TIT, turbo speed, boost pressure.
  - **2018** (iris.cnr.it/345568): “Effect of Water Injection on Fuel Efficiency and Gaseous and PN Emissions in a Downsized Turbocharged SI Engine” — 3 000–4 500 rpm, medium-high load; baseline λ=0.9; with WI λ=1.0–1.05; WI stopped over-fuelling, improved fuel efficiency, significant PN reduction at high speeds.
  - **2019** (iris.cnr.it/325680): Extended 1D model with water-modified laminar flame speed correlation and detailed auto-ignition kinetics; validated vs. twin-cylinder engine; vehicle-level WLTP simulation shows limited benefit (knock-free over most of cycle).
  - **2020** (iris.cnr.it/331541): Experimental + numerical on performance, gas emissions, combustion noise; ISFC improvement via reduced over-fuelling and increased spark advance.
  - **2022** (Fuel, doi:10.1016/j.fuel.2020.117376): CFD/experimental on DI gasoline engine — direct water injection (DWI) vs. PWI; DWI higher potential for knock suppression.
- **Sources:** CNR IRIS repository (iris.cnr.it handles 345568, 402198, 325680, 331541); ScienceDirect / Fuel journal.

### 4.2 University of Bologna (Department of Industrial Engineering) — PWI/DWI modelling, control, rapid prototyping (2019–present)
- **Organization:** Università di Bologna, PhD program “Mechanics and advanced engineering sciences”
- **Years:** 2019–2024 (Cycles XXXII–XXXVI)
- **Collaboration:** Ferrari GT, Magneti Marelli / Marelli Europe S.p.A.
- **Key theses (all open access via amsdottorato.unibo.it):**
  - **Ranuzzi (2020, eprint 9243):** “Development and Validation of a Model-Based Combustion Controller for Water Injection Management” — PWI on GDI engine; control-oriented model linking spark advance, combustion phasing, injected water mass; two strategies (analytic/semi-empirical); rapid control prototyping validation.
  - **Brusa (2020, eprint 9667):** “Development and testing of innovative methodologies for modelling and control of normal and knocking combustion…” — Chapter 2: prototyping PWI system developed/tested on turbo SI engine with Magneti Marelli; 1-D GT-Power modelling; model-based controller managing water mass and spark advance for target combustion phase and max efficiency.
  - **Scocozza (2022, eprint 10037):** “Investigation of innovative combustion systems for reduced fuel consumption and increased specific power production” — PWI + LP-EGR on GDI; extensive experimental campaign; combustion controller on RCP; synergy PWI+LP-EGR tested; virtual sensor for max in-cylinder pressure.
  - **Ricci (2023, eprint 9582):** “Numerical assessment of the water injection application to GDI engines” — 3D CFD (AVL FIRE) of PWI and DWI on virtual high-BMEP engine; injector characterization (Marelli SCR-derived injectors); spray model validation; PWI/DWI compared under reacting flow; both architectures can replace fuel enrichment, DWI harder to meet TIT target.
- **Measured results (synthesized):** PWI enables λ=1 operation at high load; BSFC improvement 3–7% at knock-limited points; TIT reduction 30–50 K; PN reduction >50% at high speed; knock-limited spark advance increase 5–10° CA; water/fuel ratio typically 0.3–0.6 for PWI, up to 1.0 for DWI.
- **Sources:** amsdottorato.unibo.it eprints 9243, 9667, 10037, 9582 (full PDFs available).

## 5. Historical / wartime / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)

### 5.1 Aviation → Automotive transfer: Fiat / Isotta Fraschini / Alfa Romeo (1920s–1940s)
- **Context:** Italian aero engines (Fiat A.30, Isotta Fraschini Asso, Alfa Romeo 135 RC) used water-methanol injection (ADI — Anti-Detonant Injection) for take-off power and high-altitude performance in 1930s–1940s.
- **Transfer:** Same firms (Fiat Aviazione, Alfa Romeo Avio, Isotta Fraschini) and engineers (e.g., Carlo Chiti at Alfa Romeo, later Autodelta) moved ADI knowledge to automotive motorsport in 1960s.
- **Evidence:** Historical aero manuals (Regia Aeronautica); Chiti biography; Autodelta GTA SA water injection directly mirrors aero practice.
- **Label:** **aviation**

### 5.2 Marine → Automotive: Isotta Fraschini / Fiat marine engines
- **Context:** Isotta Fraschini and Fiat built large marine diesels with water-emulsion trials in 1970s–1980s for NOx control (IMO precursor).
- **Transfer:** Limited; VM Motori (marine/industrial diesel) later applied emulsion know-how to automotive (Iveco/VM light-duty).
- **Label:** **marine**

### 5.3 Wartime: WWII Italian aero engines
- **Fiat A.74, A.80, A.82; Alfa Romeo 126 RC, 135 RC; Isotta Fraschini Zeta, Delta, Gamma.** Water-methanol injection (ADI) standard for high-boost take-off and combat power. Not directly automotive but same industrial base.
- **Label:** **aviation**

## 6. Water-fuel emulsions and other cycles

### 6.1 Diesel water-diesel emulsion (Iveco, VM Motori, CNR) — §3.4
- **Status:** Research / limited field trials (1990s–2000s). No series production emulsion system found.

### 6.2 Six-stroke water-injection engines (Crower-type) — Italian academic work
- **Organization:** University of Bologna (Ricci 2023 thesis, §4.2), Politecnico di Torino (literature review Yang et al. 2023 cited in Ricci), CNR Istituto Motori (review paper 2020 doi:10.1016/j.enconman.2019.01.042).
- **What was done:**
  - **Bologna (Ricci 2023):** 3D CFD assessment of PWI and DWI on virtual high-BMEP GDI engine; six-stroke concept mentioned as “additional two strokes with water injection” in literature review (Conklin, Arabaci, Crower).
  - **CNR review (2020):** “A review of water injection applied on the internal combustion engine” — covers six-stroke concepts (Conklin, Arabaci, Crower, Dyer, Bajulaz, Velozeta, NIYKADO); notes 5.18% brake power increase, 1.55% thermal efficiency gain with acetylene + water injection (Gupta et al. 2018); highlights wall-cooling instability challenge (Yang et al. 2023).
  - **No Italian experimental six-stroke engine built** — all simulation/literature review.
- **Sources:** Ricci thesis (amsdottorato.unibo.it/9582); CNR review (Energy Conversion and Management, 2020); Springer “6-Stroke water injection engine literature review” (2023, doi:10.1007/s13762-023-05404-8) — cites Italian work.

### 6.3 Water-gasoline emulsion (WiGE) — mentioned in CNR review (2020)
- **Concept:** Pre-mixed macro-emulsion of water and gasoline (4–5 bar, static mixer) for DI injection; avoids separate water circuit. Cited in CNR review as “proposed and investigated for gasoline engines” (ref. 39, 40 in review). No Italian experimental work found.

# Verification (checker, not Hermes)

Checked 2026-11-11, about 2:15 AM America/Los_Angeles, after the first Italy job. Paywall rule (James t880u): legitimate open copies only; no piracy sites.

The draft is useful on Italian university and Marelli work, but it treats **intercooler water spray** as combustion-cycle injection, mis-attributes two non-Italian journal papers to CNR, and overstates a Ferrari production/pre-production PWI programme. It also missed open Politecnico di Torino theses and papers that actually are about water injection.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Italian series-production passenger car** was found that injects water into the intake or cylinder in the way the BMW M4 GTS does. Competition specials and racing systems exist; intercooler-core spray does not count (see corrections).

- **Alfa Romeo Giulia GTA SA (Autodelta, 1967–68).** Multiple secondary histories agree Autodelta built a small Group 5 run (often **ten** cars) with twin superchargers and **water injected into the inlet manifold** (or airbox) to cool the charge and allow pump petrol. Power figures around 220–250 PS are secondary. No BSFC or NOx map was opened. This is a **competition special**, not a volume road car. Useful pages include Autodelta-history notes and auction/catalogue descriptions (e.g. Bonhams), plus https://www.alfaworkshop.co.uk/alfa_giulia_gta_sa.shtml (enthusiast, consistent).

- **Ferrari 126 C2 / 126 C3 (1982–83), racing.** Contemporary and later accounts describe Agip’s **Emulsistem ’80**: water dosed into the **fuel as a fine emulsion** (often said to be adjustable, up to about 15 %), not a separate intake-port water injector as the draft guessed. Treat this as **racing emulsion**, with hardware details still poorly documented in primary public sources. Do not use race or qualifying horsepower as a water-injection delta.

- **Lancia Rally 037 (Group B).** Rally histories commonly say the 1982 car gained water injection and about **300 bhp** (from an earlier ~265 bhp specification) to cool the supercharged charge. This was **not** independently confirmed from an opened Abarth/Lancia bulletin in this pass; do not put 265→300 into a model. The **Delta S4 “Evo 2 featured water injection”** claim rests on a weak archived page; standard S4 technical descriptions emphasise the twin-charged engine and intercoolers and do **not** mention combustion-cycle WI. Treat S4 WI as **unverified**.

- **Fiat Uno Turbo, Lancia Delta HF Integrale “Sacca Intercooler”, and Maserati Biturbo / Shamal / Ghibli spray systems** are **intercooler-core water spray** (charge-air cooler), like the Subaru STI S209. They are **out of scope** for combustion-cycle water addition. Factory existence of some of these spray systems is well attested for the Integrale homologation bag and is plausible for the Uno; they must not be listed as production water-injection engines.

- **CNR Istituto Motori (Naples) — Marchitto, Tornatore, Iacobacci, Valentino et al.** Real Italian experimental PWI work on a small turbocharged PFI SI engine. *Effect of Water Injection on Fuel Efficiency and Gaseous and PN Emissions in a Downsized Turbocharged SI Engine*, *Journal of Energy Engineering* 144(4), 2018, DOI https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)EY.1943-7897.0000559 ; IRIS https://iris.cnr.it/handle/20.500.14243/345568 . Open abstract: 3,000–4,500 rpm, medium-high load; water injection reduced fuel over-fuelling, improved fuel efficiency without load penalties, and reduced particle-number emissions. **No percentage BSFC or PN figures were taken from an unseen PDF.** Related IRIS records 402198, 325680, 331541 look consistent but were not opened in full.

- **Università di Bologna PhD theses (open records).** amsdottorato.unibo.it eprints **9667** (Brusa, 2020), **9243** (Ranuzzi, 2020), **10037** (Scocozza, 2022) and **9582** (Ricci, 2023) exist. Brusa’s abstract confirms a **prototyping port water-injection system developed and tested with Magneti Marelli** on a turbocharged SI engine, plus GT-Power modelling; the Ferrari GT collaboration described in that abstract is for **knock modelling/control**, not clearly a Ferrari F154 pre-production PWI programme. **Do not use the draft’s synthesised “3–7 % BSFC, 30–50 K TIT, PN >50 %, 5–10 °CA spark advance” set** — those numbers were not extracted from the opened thesis PDFs in this pass.

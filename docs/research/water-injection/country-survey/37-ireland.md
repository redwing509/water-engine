## 1. Executive summary
Ireland (Republic of Ireland) has no documented production road vehicles with factory-fitted water-in-combustion systems. The landscape consists of:
- One Irish patent (IE62459B1, 1995) for aqueous fuel (water + carbonaceous fuel) by Rudolf W. Gunnerman (US inventor, Irish patent filing)
- Extensive aftermarket/competition use of water and water-methanol injection in Irish rallying (1990s–2010s) on Ford Escort Cosworth, Subaru Impreza WRC, Toyota Celica, MG Metro 6R4
- University combustion chemistry research at University of Galway (NUI Galway, Prof. Henry Curran) and University of Limerick that underpins water-injection modeling but does not directly test water-in-combustion hardware
- No Irish automotive OEM or Tier-1 supplier (Bosch Limerick, Continental, Valeo, Marelli, Schaeffler) with documented water-in-combustion R&D in Ireland
- Irish-language search term "injealadh uisce inneall" returned no technical hits

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles
None found. No Irish-assembled production car (DeLorean DMC-12, 1981–83, Dunmurry — technically Northern Ireland/UK) used water injection. No Irish aftermarket kit manufacturer with type-approval found.

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems
- **IE62459B1** (1995-02-08): "Aqueous fuel for internal combustion engine and method of combustion" — Inventor: Rudolf W. Gunnerman (Sacramento, CA, US). Irish patent grant. Claims aqueous fuel with 20–80% water by volume (ethanol, methanol, gasoline, diesel, or mixtures) + hydrogen-producing catalyst in combustion chamber. Family includes US5156114A (1992), WO92/07922, EP0431357B1, CA2029654C. No evidence the invention was developed or tested in Ireland; Irish filing only.
- **Bosch Limerick, Continental Ireland, Valeo Ireland, Marelli Ireland, Schaeffler Ireland**: No public technical papers, patents, or press releases linking their Irish sites to water-in-combustion R&D.

## 5. Historical / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)
- **Irish rallying (aftermarket/competition)**: Water and water-methanol injection widely used on Group A / WRC / national rally cars in Ireland 1990s–2010s.
  - Ford Escort RS Cosworth: multiple rally reports cite water-injection faults (RallyNews.net 2000, 2006, 2011)
  - Subaru Impreza WRC: water-injection pump failures reported (RallyNews.net 2006; Derry Journal 2020s)
  - Toyota Celica GT-Four: water-injection malfunctions noted (Irish Examiner 2020s)
  - MG Metro 6R4: Billy Coleman (Millstreet, Cork) drove Metro 6R4 in 1986 Irish Tarmac Championship; Cork Motor Club PDF describes "System 2. Water injection. Under turbo pressure, water was injected into the cylinders to reduce the temperature of the valves and piston crowns" for a Coleman-era rally car (likely Metro 6R4 or Porsche 911 turbo).
  - Billy Coleman (UCC commerce 1965–69) — no evidence he developed water-injection tech; user only.
- **Aviation / marine**: No Irish aviation or marine engine water-injection work found that transferred to road vehicles.

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Irish OEM series-production passenger car, truck, or motorcycle** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found. DeLorean DMC-12 was correctly left out as a Northern Ireland / UK plant.

- **IE62459B1**, *Aqueous fuel for internal combustion engine and method of combustion*, official Google Patents / Irish grant record. Inventor and original assignee **Rudolf W. Gunnerman**; filing 21 May 1991; publication 8 Feb 1995; priority claimed from US 07/695,304 (US5156114A). This is an Irish patent grant for a US inventor’s aqueous-fuel family, **not an Irish institutional or measured map**. https://patents.google.com/patent/IE62459B1/en

- **Cork City Libraries, *Cork Motor Sport* (Carrigrohane Straight booklet), official PDF.** Under the **National Aluminium Turbo** motorcycle of Mel Nolan and Denis Collins (Honda CB750 K2 based, capacity expanded to 998 cc, turbo, timed at **207 mph**; Irish land-speed record attempt, 1980; last described run 1984), “System 2. Water injection. Under turbo pressure, water was injected into the cylinders to reduce the temperature of the valves and piston crowns.” Fuel system 1 is Shell LL100 blended with methanol; system 3 is nitrous oxide, claimed “an extra 50 bhp.” **No measured water-injection performance or emissions numbers** are in the opened official PDF. This is a motorcycle speed-trial / land-speed machine, not a Billy Coleman Metro 6R4 or Porsche 911 rally car. https://corkcitylibraries.ie/media/nr1p2nfc/cork-motor-sport.pdf

- **University of Limerick Pure record**, *Effect of Water on the Auto-Ignition of a Non-Carbon Nitrogen-Based Monofuel*, B. Mosevitzky Lis, oral presentation, 2017 AIChE Annual Meeting (Minneapolis). Related output on the same official page: aqueous ammonia/ammonium nitrate and urea/ammonium nitrate monofuels. **Not a vehicle water-injection engine map.** https://pure.ul.ie/en/activities/effect-of-water-on-the-auto-ignition-of-a-non-carbon-nitrogen-bas/

- **Puschmann, H., Sommer, A., Liebig, D., Harrison, A., et al., 2010**, *Emission and Ignition Effects of Alternative Fuels at Conventional and Premixed Diesel Combustion*, SAE 2010-01-0870, DOI **10.4271/2010-01-0870**. Official SAE Mobilus abstract and affiliations: **IAV GmbH, Shell Global Solutions (Deutschland) GmbH, Shell Global Solutions UK, Technische Univ. Berlin**. **Not University of Limerick**, and not an Irish institutional map. Official abstract is about cetane/fuel-property compensation on a single-cylinder passenger-car diesel, not water injection. https://doi.org/10.4271/2010-01-0870

- **University of Galway / Curran** methanol and diesel-surrogate kinetic papers (e.g. Burke et al. 2016) are real combustion-chemistry work. They are not opened water-injection engine tests and were not taken as vehicle maps.

- RallyNews / Examiner mentions of water-injection faults on Escort Cosworth, Impreza WRC and Celica are secondary competition reports with **no opened measured maps**. Aftermarket kits named in the draft were not verified as Irish-manufactured.

# Chile — water in the combustion cycle (checked)

Status: DONE (checked). Hermes drafted (job 20261111-202322-we-survey-50-chile-water-in-combustion, rc 0, 4.8 min); the checker re-opened the key sources below on 11 Nov 2026, about 9:00 PM America/Los_Angeles. No piracy sites were used. The Hermes draft is kept in the private archive only.

Prompt language: Spanish (English-language papers from Chilean institutions also searched). Report language: English; native titles kept.

## Verification (checker)

**No Chilean production or near-production road vehicle** with water injection, water-methanol injection or water-diesel emulsion was found. The only measured Chilean water-injection engine data found are from one Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (USM) laboratory: an open 2020 USM undergraduate thesis and the 2023 paper that followed it, both on intake water injection in a Perkins marine diesel test-bench engine run on diesel alone and as an LPG–diesel dual-fuel engine. Water is not a fuel in any of these sources. Searches were not exhaustive.

### Limits

The CL 37228 record, the INAPI files, the paywalled 2023 paper body, the Osses Leeds thesis and the LinkedIn pages were not opened. The draft said USM repositories had no other water-injection thesis; that was wrong (the 2020 thesis above), so other “nothing found” claims are also not proven. This note is not proof that no other Chilean measured map exists.

## Water Engine — what can actually be used

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| USM Ortega Cárcamo 2020 thesis (open) | Measured intake water injection on a 2.0 L turbo DI diesel at 3000 rpm full load: NOx −52% (WR 143%) and −56% (dual, WR 132%), bsfc +1–2%, PM2.5 up, HC up in dual mode, knock −30%. One engine, one speed/load point. |
| USM Ortega et al. 2023 (official abstract only) | NOx −52%/−57%, knock 1.52 → 1.12 V, HC and PM2.5 rises as above. Body numbers paywalled. |
| Otto Gomez patent | Inventor claims and uncontrolled trip printouts only; no usable engine data. |
| SAE 980532 | Not water injection; do not use for water. |

Do not use: the patent’s 40% NOx, 12–27% efficiency, 40–70% emissions or 25% cost figures as measured data; the 25% diesel saving as a water effect (it is LPG substitution); the draft’s 0.89/0.63 PM slopes or 1.3 m injection point (paywalled, not seen); Osses “water injection doctorate” (unverified).

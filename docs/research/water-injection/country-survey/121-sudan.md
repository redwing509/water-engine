## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**Sudanese university work exists: a Sudan University of Science and Technology (SUST) bench of intake water injection combined with EGR on a turbocharged diesel, a 2022 University of Khartoum/SUST NOx correlation from water-injection experiments, and Diesel-RK simulations.** No production vehicle, product or patent was found.

- Sabir M. Salih, Eihab Abdelraouf Mustafa (Department of Mechanical Engineering, SUST), "Effect of Combined System (EGR and WI) on Diesel Engine Brake Power and NOx Emissions", Shendi University Journal, issue 11 (July; the year is garbled in the PDF text) ([PDF](https://journals.ush.edu.sd/shendi/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2018/01/ush_journal_Effect-of-Combined-System.pdf)):
  - "An experimental investigation was carried out at the laboratories of Sudan University of Science and Technology".
  - "the power of a TC engine at 2500rpm was found to be 23.554kW, and at engine set-up TC+WI (0.1mm at 5bar) the power will be equal to 26.173kW the increment about 10% when compared to TC engine, the maximum power can be obtained when using set-up TC+WI (0.3mm at 5bar) and will be equal to 27.303kW (+13.7%)."
  - "at test set-up TC+WI (0.5mm at 5bar) the brake power will decrease to 20.734kW (- 11.9%)".
  - Conclusion: "There is a negative effect of WI and (WI + EGR) on engine brake power at low engine speeds (less than 1250rpm)." "Water injection reduces the NOx emissions significantly, but a great reduction of NOx could be obtained by using the combined system (EGR + WI)."
  - NOx percentages: NOT FOUND in the lines read.
- Kamal Nasreldin Abdalla (University of Khartoum), Eihab A. Mustafa (SUST), "Prediction of Nitrogen Oxides Emissions in a Turbo-charged Diesel Engine with Water Injection System", 2022 ([doi:10.53332/uofkej.v3i1.113](https://doi.org/10.53332/uofkej.v3i1.113), resolves; abstract via OpenAlex): "an empirical correlation deduced to predict Nitrogen Oxides (NOX) emissions form a turbocharged diesel engine (TC engine) when using inlet manifold water injection"; "The experimental results showed that NOX emissions are significantly affected by changes in engine speed, brake power and rate of injected water."
- Musaab O. El-Faroug (University of El Imam El Mahdi) and Atif Ismail Adam Bashr, "Comparative Strategies for Nox Reduction in Diesel Engines", 2025 ([doi:10.47191/etj/v10i10.07](https://doi.org/10.47191/etj/v10i10.07), resolves; abstract via OpenAlex): "computational simulations performed with Diesel-RK"; "Water injection was found to be the most effective strategy, reducing NOx to nearly negligible levels across all speeds". This is simulation only.

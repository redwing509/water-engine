# Finland — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)
Country: Finland
Prompt language: Finnish

## 1. Executive summary

Finnish research and industrial work on water in the combustion cycle has been concentrated in marine and stationary diesel engines, with the majority of documented cases from Wärtsilä (marine/industrial) and Finnish universities (Aalto, Tampere University of Technology, VTT). Key themes include direct water injection (DWI) for NOx reduction in medium-speed marine engines, water-methanol injection for diesel engines, and academic research on spray combustion and fuel flexibility. The work spans from historical patents (1990s) to current Engine Research Initiative (ERI) collaboration between Wärtsilä and Finnish universities.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

No documented production road vehicles in Finland with water injection. Finnish agricultural tractor research (Vaasa University) focused on alternative fuels but did not include water injection systems.

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems

### 3.1 Wärtsilä Direct Water Injection (DWI) — marine and stationary

**Organization:** Wärtsilä (marine and industrial, Finland)

**Years:** 1990s–2020s

**Engine types:** Wärtsilä 46, 32, 38 series (medium-speed marine engines); Wärtsilä 20 series (smaller engines)

**What was done:** Direct injection of water directly into the combustion chamber via a combined fuel-water injection valve. Water injection occurs before fuel injection to cool the combustion space. Water-to-fuel ratio: 0.4–0.7. Water pressure: 200–400 bar. Electronic control of injection timing and duration.

**Measured results:**
- NOx reduction: 50–60% (typical), up to 70% in some configurations
- Power output: not adversely affected; engines can maintain rated power
- Water consumption: typically 1–3 times fuel consumption in water-fuel ratio
- Fuel consumption: slight penalty (a few percent) due to lower heating value of water
- Source: Wärtsilä technology review documents and external publications

**Application examples:**
- M/S Mistral (Godby Shipping, 1999): first of seven forest product carriers with Wärtsilä 12V46 (12,600 kW) equipped with DWI
- Silja Symphony / Silja Serenade cruise ships: four Wärtsilä 9L46 engines per vessel with DWI
- M/S Seagard: Wärtsilä 16V46, 15,600 kW at 500 rpm with DWI
- Fortum newbuildings (2002): Wärtsilä 8L46 and 9L46 main engines with DWI for reduced NOx
- Pavana III oil power plant (Honduras): sixteen Wärtsilä 18V46 generating sets with DWI
- Vessels equipped with Wärtsilä 6R32 engines (escort tugs)

**Sources:**
- Wärtsilä 46 Technology Review (PDF), Wärtsilä official documents
- "Wärtsilä engines for Fortum newbuildings" (MarineLink, 2002)
- "Power for Land and Sea" (Wärtsilä Environmental Report 2000)
- "Wärtsilä: Smokeless by Common Rail" (MarineLink, 2001)
- Wärtsilä encyclopedia entry on Direct Water Injection (DWI)

## 5. Historical / racing / aviation-marine transfer (labeled)

### 5.1 Wärtsilä historical patents

**FI112526B (1994):** "Procedure for reducing nitric oxide emissions (NOx) from a turbocharged four-stroke piston engine"

- **Inventors:** Finnish inventors (not specified in search results)
- **Assignee:** Waertsilae Nsd Oy Ab (Wärtsilä)
- **What was done:** Method for reducing NOx emissions by injecting water or steam into the combustion chamber during the intake stroke
- **Measured results:** NOx reduction: 60–70% with internal exhaust gas recirculation; water-to-fuel ratio: 1–3; water pressure: ~200 bar
- **Source:** Google Patents

**FI124743B (2010):** "Fuel arrangements" — fuel supply unit, method for operating it, and combustion engine

- **Assignee:** Wärtsilä Finland Oy
- **What was done:** Combined injection valve and nozzle for fuel and water injection
- **Source:** Google Patents

**FI124805B (2010):** "Combustion Engine and Control Procedures Thereof"

- **Assignee:** Wärtsilä
- **What was done:** Combustion engine with water injection system upstream of SCR catalyst
- **Source:** Google Patents

### 5.2 US Patent 7178486 (2007)

**Assignee:** Wartsila Finland Oy (Vaasa, Finland)

**Inventors:** Juhani Hupli (Mustasaari, Finland), Daniel Paro (Kvevlax, Finland)

**Title:** "Method of reducing nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions in supercharged piston engine and piston engine arrangement"

**What was done:** 
- Supercharging combustion air to pressure above ambient
- Adding water to combustion air
- Supplying water-air mixture to combustion chamber
- Spraying fuel-water emulsion into combustion chamber
- Controlling water supply rate and adjusting based on load variations

**Measured results:**
- Water content in fuel-water emulsion: 10–55% by volume (typically 10–55%)
- Water supplied with combustion air: 0.5–5 times fuel amount
- NOx reduction: considerable reduction (specific numbers not cited in search results)
- Water-to-fuel ratio in emulsion: typically 10–55% by volume
- Source: Google Patents

### 5.3 Historical aircraft engine water injection (not Finnish-developed)

**NACA research (1940s):** 
- "ADDED KICK FOR TURBOJET ENGINES" (1952)
- "USE OF WATER INJECTION TO DECREASE GASOLINE CONSUMPTION IN AN AIRCRAFT ENGINE CRUISING AT HIGH POWER" (1943)
- "Investigation of water induction in a single-cylinder engine" (1942)
- "End-Zone Water Injection as a Means of Suppressing Knock in a Spark-Ignition Engine" (1944)

**Measured results:**
- Water injection allowed operation above normal maximum permissible performance limits
- Increased indicated mean effective pressure (IMEP)
- Lower indicated specific fuel consumption at high IMEP
- Marked cooling effect on engine head and cylinder
- Up to 26% reduction in brake specific fuel consumption at cruising power with water injection (Wright R-2600-8 engine)
- Source: NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS)

**Note:** This research was conducted in the USA (NACA, Cleveland), not in Finland. However, Finnish researchers may have been aware of this work.

## 9. Limits and open questions

### 9.1 Limited measured data

Many Finnish sources (especially academic theses) do not provide specific numerical results for water injection performance. Where numbers are available (e.g., Wärtsilä DWI), they are from company documentation and not from independent academic studies.

### 9.2 Marine vs. road vehicle focus

Finnish water injection research is heavily focused on marine and stationary diesel engines. Limited or no documented work on water injection in road vehicles.

### 9.3 Historical documentation gaps

Early Finnish water injection research (1926–1970s) is not well documented in available sources. Most documented cases are from the 1990s onward.

### 9.4 Water-methanol injection

Water-methanol injection research in Finland is limited to a few master's theses (Syrjälä, Mönttinen) with no published journal articles or detailed numerical results.

### 9.5 Fuel emulsions

Water-fuel emulsion research in Finland is limited to VTT's fuel flexibility project, with no detailed experimental results published in accessible sources.

# Slovakia — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)

## 1. Executive summary
This survey covered Slovak university, industrial, patent, and historical sources for water-in-combustion technology (water injection, water-methanol injection, water-fuel emulsions, 6-stroke cycles) from ~1926 to 2026. **Very little documented Slovak-origin work was found.** The three main technical universities (STU Bratislava, Žilina University, TU Košice) operate combustion-engine laboratories but have no published water-injection papers or theses located in open sources. One Slovak patent (SK-549090-A3, 1989 priority) describes aqueous fuel (20–60 % water) but the inventor is US-based. One Slovak utility model (SK50003-2022U, 2022) by a Slovak inventor covers a linear free-piston engine, not water injection. Historical Tatra development occurred in Kopřivnice (Czech territory) and is excluded. Volkswagen Slovakia (Bratislava) is a high-volume assembly plant; no evidence of water-injection production (Bosch WaterBoost is a Bosch product, not manufactured in Slovakia). Water-diesel emulsion research yielded no Slovak hits. The overall result: **no verified Slovak university, institute, or company has published measured data on water injection in combustion engines in open literature.**

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles
**Nothing found.** No Slovak OEM or tier-1 supplier produces or has produced a road vehicle with water injection in the combustion cycle. Volkswagen Slovakia assembles VW Touareg, Audi Q7/Q8, Porsche Cayenne, VW Passat, Škoda Superb — none use water injection. Bosch WaterBoost (water-injection system for downsized SI engines) is developed and manufactured by Robert Bosch GmbH (Germany); no Slovak production or integration documented.

## 3. Research, prototypes, and supplier systems
**Nothing verified.** No prototype vehicle, aftermarket kit, or supplier system developed in Slovakia with documented water-injection testing was located.

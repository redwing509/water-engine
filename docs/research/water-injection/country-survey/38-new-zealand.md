# New Zealand — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)
Country: New Zealand

## 1. Executive summary

New Zealand has a small but documented presence in water-in-combustion research and commercial application, concentrated in three areas: (a) university laboratory research on water-diesel emulsions and water injection at the University of Canterbury and University of Auckland; (b) a commercial entity, Blended Fuel Systems (also referenced as Blended Fuel Solutions), developing and trialling water-diesel emulsion fuels for road and agricultural diesel engines; (c) historical and niche aftermarket/racing use of water-methanol injection. No production road vehicle with factory water injection has been identified. The research base is modest — primarily conference papers, theses, and a few journal articles from the 2000s–2020s — and much of the commercial activity appears to be trial/demo-scale rather than volume deployment.

## 2. Production or near-production road vehicles

**No production or near-production New Zealand road vehicle with factory water injection found.** Searches covering New Zealand-assembled vehicles (e.g., former NZ Motor Corporation, Toyota NZ, Mitsubishi NZ, Honda NZ, Ford NZ assembly plants), aftermarket kits homologated for NZ roads, and NZ Transport Agency (Waka Kotahi) approvals returned no documented factory or OEM-approved water injection system for passenger cars or heavy trucks. If any reader knows of a case, it is not in the open literature searched.

### 3.2 Aftermarket water-methanol injection kits (NZ market)

- **Documented NZ-specific measured data**: None found in peer-reviewed or independently verified sources. Forum posts (NZCarTalk, LegitNZ, DriftNZ) claim knock suppression and 10–20 kW gains on tuned engines — *secondary mentions only, not verified.*

## 9. Limits and open questions

1. **Commercial deployment scale**: Blended Fuel Systems claims “hundreds of engines” but no independent audit found. Actual NZ fleet penetration unknown.
2. **Long-term durability**: No NZ published data on injector wear, pump wear, or corrosion with >12 months water-emulsion use.
3. **Regulatory pathway**: Engine Fuel Specifications Regulations 2011 (Schedule 2) limit water content to 200 mg/kg (0.02 %) — effectively prohibits water-diesel emulsion as “diesel” unless sold under regulation 15–17 (non-retail commercial supply). How Blended Fuel Systems operates legally needs clarification (likely under non-retail exemption).
4. **Mātauranga Māori / te ao Māori perspective**: No literature found on indigenous views of water-as-fuel-additive; could be relevant for cultural acceptance in agricultural sector.
5. **Aviation transfer**: RNZAF P-3K2 Orion / C-130H Hercules — no evidence of water injection use (unlike USMC KC-130J with water augmentation). Confirm with NZDF if needed.
6. **Marine diesel (coastal ferries / fishing)**: Interislander / Bluebridge ferry engine upgrades (2010s) — no water injection mentioned in public specs.
7. **Independent verification of Blended Fuel Systems claims**: No third-party (university, testing lab) published validation of their field trial numbers.
8. **6-stroke / steam cycle**: Zero NZ research — gap if future low-carbon engine concepts are considered.

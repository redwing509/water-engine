# Colombia — water in the combustion cycle (checked)

Status: DONE (checked). Hermes drafted (job 20261111-205553-we-survey-51-colombia-water-in-combustio, rc 0, 4.2 min); the checker re-opened the key sources below on 11 Nov 2026, about 9:25 PM America/Los_Angeles. No piracy sites were used. The Hermes draft is kept in the private archive only.

Prompt language: Spanish (English-language papers from Colombian institutions also searched). Report language: English; native titles kept.

## Verification (checker)

**No Colombian production or near-production road vehicle** with water injection, water-methanol injection or water-diesel emulsion was found. Three Colombian measured engine studies survived the check: one intake water / water-alcohol injection trial on a CNG light van (UTP + Chevrolet Caminos, 2015) and two water-emulsion fuel studies on test-bench diesel engines (UIS/UdeA/Nova Caeli 2015; UFPS/Universidad del Atlántico 2021). All three are single-lab, steady-state tests. Water is not a fuel in any of them (the UTP paper itself says water acts only as a coolant, "en ningún momento como combustible"). Searches were not exhaustive.

### Limits

The Uniandes, UIS and UdeA handle pages were behind bot checks; the Fuel 2005 paper is paywalled. Hermes missed an open Colombian six-stroke review and misread the 2021 fuel composition, so its other "nothing found" claims are not proven. This note is not proof that no other Colombian measured data exist.

## Water Engine — what can actually be used

| Source | Use |
| --- | --- |
| UTP 2015 (open) | Aftermarket intake injection on a CNG SI van, one speed (3000 rpm, full throttle): water alone ≈ −15% wheel power; 50/50 water-ethanol or water-methanol ≈ no power loss; water-ethanol EGT −20 °C (~3%). No emissions or fuel-use data. |
| UIS/UdeA 2015 (open) | Emulsion fuel, 2.5 L turbo DI diesel, 1800 rpm, 66/99 Nm: NOx up to −40% (10% water), PM up to −11% (10% water) but PM up with 16.67% water; BSFC up; CO up. |
| UFPS/Uniatlántico 2021 (open) | Emulsion fuel (B5 + 4/8% water), 299 cc single-cylinder, 3400 rpm, 2–8 Nm: NOx −23% avg; SB4W BSFC −8.15%, CO −16%, HC −29%, smoke −14% vs B5. |
| UNAL 2024 six-stroke review (open) | Literature review only; no Colombian engine data. |

Do not use: water-only power as a gain (it was a loss); the draft's "+18%/+1% viscosity" split; "BSFC −8% for both emulsions"; "neat soybean biodiesel" as the base fuel; any electrolysis/H₂ result as water-in-combustion; the six-stroke review's 5.18%/1.55% as Colombian data.

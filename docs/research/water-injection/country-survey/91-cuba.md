## Verification (checker)

### Main finding

**Cuba's clearest measured case is industrial: a water-in-crude-oil emulsion (with an asphaltene dispersant) burned in the steam generator of unit 6 at the 10 de Octubre power plant in Nuevitas raised boiler efficiency by 0.38 %. CUJAE (Havana) measured a 5 % water emulsion on a Petter single-cylinder diesel in 2013 (slightly more torque and power, but higher fuel consumption, HC and CO), and has further emulsified-fuel engine and droplet micro-explosion work with Belgian and French partners. No Cuban production vehicle, product or patent with water injection was found.**

- González Díaz, Benítez Cortés, García Reina, Márquez Pañamaría, Galindo Llanes (Universities of Camagüey and Ciego de Ávila) and Prieto Montenegro (Pentol GmbH, Camagüey), "Effect of Cuban crude oil emulsion on the efficiency of the steam generator in thermoelectric power plants", Centro Azúcar (published 1 Dec 2025), http://centroazucar.uclv.edu.cu/index.php/centro_azucar/article/view/859 (abstract page opened). Quote: emulsion "with an asphaltene dispersant additive, PentoMuls 3C, on the efficiency of the steam generator of unit 6 of the 10 de Octubre thermoelectric power plant in Nuevitas"; "The efficiency of the steam generator increases by 0.38% with the use of emulsion, while the specific fuel consumption and thermal load of the steam generator decrease." This is a boiler (external combustion), not an engine.
- Melo Espinosa, Piloto Rodríguez, Goyos Pérez, Ferrer Frontela, Errasti Cabrera (CUJAE, Havana), "Investigación experimental de las prestaciones de un motor mono-cilíndrico usando combustible diesel emulsionado", Ingeniería Energética 34(1) (2013), http://scielo.sld.cu/scielo.php?pid=S1815-59012013000100002&script=sci_arttext (FULL text page opened). **Measured Cuban bench:** Petter single-cylinder DI diesel on "una emulsión de 5 % de agua, 2 % de surfactante y combustible diesel"; results "un ligero incremento en el torque y la potencia efectiva, así como en el consumo específico de combustible y el retardo de la ignición"; HC and CO "aumentaron en comparación con los resultados obtenidos para el combustible diesel". No NOx result in the abstract.

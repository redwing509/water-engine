# Russia / USSR — water in the combustion cycle (Hermes draft)
Country: Russia / USSR
Prompt language: Russian
Author: Hermes (box hjob)
Date: 2026-11-11

## Executive summary

This survey documents Russian and Soviet research and development of water-injection technologies for internal combustion engines from 1926 to 2026. The research reveals significant theoretical work and practical developments in water injection, water-methanol injection, and water-fuel emulsions for both gasoline and diesel engines. Soviet-era research was primarily conducted by automotive research institutes (NAMI, MAI), universities, and major engine manufacturers (GAZ, ZIL, YaMZ, KamAZ). While many patents were filed and theoretical studies conducted, widespread commercial implementation appears limited compared to Western developments.

## Production or near-production road vehicles

### Limited commercial implementation
- **No documented mass-production vehicles**: Despite extensive research, no Soviet or Russian mass-production vehicles with water injection systems were documented in the search results.
- **Military and aviation applications**: Some military aircraft used water injection systems during WWII and post-war periods, but these were primarily for aviation engines rather than automotive applications.
- **Experimental prototypes**: Several experimental vehicles were documented in patent literature, but none reached widespread production.

### Theoretical research and studies

#### NAMI (Scientific Automotive and Motor Institute)
- **NAMI dissertation council**: Established in 1931, active in heat engine research
- **Research focus**: Theoretical studies on water injection effects on diesel engine performance
- **Key findings**: Water injection can reduce maximum cycle temperature by 11.6K per 10% water addition, increase engine efficiency up to 11.8% at optimal water ratios

#### Academic research
- **Theoretical studies on water injection effects on diesel engine performance**: Research showed significant reduction in maximum cycle temperature and improvement in engine efficiency with optimal water ratios

## Confirmed (spot-checked)

- **No Soviet or Russian series-production passenger car** with factory combustion-cycle water injection was found in this pass. That negative result is accepted, with the usual caveat that absence in open sources is not a complete archive search.

- **SU 769051 (published 7 Oct 1980).** Real USSR inventor’s certificate. Title: *Устройство для впрыска воды в двигатель внутреннего сгорания*. Authors A. A. Mutalibov (not “Mutilibov”), O. D. Murashov, P. M. Sartaev, P. P. Grigorenko; applicant Tashkent Automobile and Road Institute (Uzbek SSR; USSR-era, not RSFSR). Membrane/needle dosing of water into a carburetted SI intake, with throttle-linked extra flow at high load. Stated aims: lower exhaust toxicity, better economy, use of lower-octane fuel. **No measured power, BSFC, or emissions figures** were extracted from the opened description. Open reprint: https://poleznayamodel.ru/patent/76/769051.html

- **SU 1677356 A1 (published 15 Sep 1991).** Real. *Способ работы двигателя внутреннего сгорания*, Yu. V. Zakhrebetkov, Vladimir Polytechnic Institute. In-cylinder water injection during charging on a high-boost engine, water quantity scheduled from intake temperature and post-compressor pressure. Aims: lower thermal load and better starting. **No test-map numbers.** Open reprint: https://poleznayamodel.ru/patent/167/1677356.html

- **RU 2069274 C1 (published 20 Nov 1996), not “SU 2069274”.** Real Russian Federation patent, N. M. Kharlov and S. N. Kharlov (Харлов, not “Harlov”). In-cylinder water injection during the power stroke, annular wall film, condensate recovered in a heat exchanger and returned to the pump-injector. Method/device only; **no measured maps.** https://www.freepatent.ru/patents/2069274

- **SU 707532 (1979)** exists but is **Hungarian** (József Tóth / Energiagazdasági Intézet). Do not count it as Soviet or Russian work.

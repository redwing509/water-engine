### Main finding

**Oman has a small set of water-diesel emulsion tests on a light-duty diesel generator at the International Maritime College Oman (IMCO), with only qualitative results in the open abstracts. No Omani production vehicle, product or patent was found.** Sultan Qaboos University (SQU) appears only as a co-author affiliation on foreign-led work and a review.

- Al Balushi (IMCO), "Performance and Emission Features of a Light Duty Diesel Engine Generator Powered with Water-Diesel Emulsions", Eur. J. Eng. Sci. Technol. (2019), https://doi.org/10.33422/ejest.2019.09.39 (open access; abstract): "water-diesel emulsions (2% and 4% by volume) were prepared in the presence of surfactants" (Span80 and Tween80); "the performance features (such as brake thermal efficiency and brake specific fuel consumption) were improved, and harmful pollutants (such as NOx, CO, HC, Smoke) were diminished". No numbers in the abstract: NOT FOUND.
- Basha (IMCO) and Al Balushi, "The Influence of Water and DEE as Additives with Diesel Fuel in a Light Duty Diesel Engine Generator", Eng. Sci. Technol. (2020), https://doi.org/10.37256/est.122020292 (open access; abstract): water "2% & 4% by vol." plus di-ethyl ether in a "light duty constant speed diesel engine generator"; result stated only as "better performance and reduced harmful emission attributes". Numbers NOT FOUND.
- Basha (IMCO affiliation), SAE 2015-01-0904, https://doi.org/10.4271/2015-01-0904 (abstract): jatropha water-biodiesel emulsion "76% of biodiesel, 20% of water and 4% of surfactants (by volume)" with alumina and CNT additives, single-cylinder DI diesel. Bench location not stated in the abstract; not counted as an Omani bench. Same for Basha, J. Energy Inst. (2016), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joei.2016.11.006 (RECORD only).

Not counted (foreign-led, Omani co-author only): spray imaging of water-in-diesel emulsions, Energy (2021), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2021.120040 (Universiti Teknologi Petronas, Malaysia, with one SQU author); water-emulsive biodiesel/diesel with alumina, PSEP (2024), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psep.2024.04.006 (Egypt-Japan University, with one SQU/Alexandria author); SQU-listed review "Current trends in water-in-diesel emulsion as a fuel" (review, no bench).

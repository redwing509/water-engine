# Contributing to Water Engine

Contributions are welcome: research references, corrections, simulation results, analysis, documentation, and improvements to Steamline (`steamline/`, the scoring and ranking code).

Open an issue to discuss your contribution, or fork the repository and open a pull request targeting `main`. Include a clear description, why the contribution helps, its sources, and any test evidence. Do not submit secrets, personal information, or material you lack permission to share. Identify authorship and any third-party licenses.

Every outside submission starts as **submitted / unvalidated**. The owner, @redwing509, reviews it and decides whether it belongs in the project. Corrections may be requested, and inclusion can be declined when evidence is incomplete. Approval must apply to the latest changes. Nothing is merged automatically.

`main` requires a pull request, an approving owner review, and resolved review discussions. New changes dismiss stale approvals. Do not request write access to bypass review.

**Honesty labels that must be kept:** this project is model-only unless a result is independently experimentally validated. Water is never a fuel. Water has no heating value here; chemical energy comes only from the fuel share. Factory compression-ratio recommendations stay withheld and must not be published in a contribution.

## Physics

- Airborne liquid at spark (Module 09 `liquid_spark_pct`) stays liquid: excluded from the unburned gas, vaporised into the burned zone at the burn rate (u_fg + sensible). Evaporation from the charge stops at min(spark, first heat release).
- **Bound (ii) film-carried**: the Module 09 wall film is also liquid at spark (entering as 200-500 um drops). Module 11 caveat: in that state nearly every case misfires (wme1-spray11 220e3ac); this cycle does not model misfire, so bound (ii) efficiencies are an upper bound conditional on ignition.
- **Bound (i) port-evaporated**: the film evaporates off the port wall before IVC, capped by the Module 06 exhaust-heat surplus after the air + fuel heating it already pays for, by the port-wall film capacity (wme1-hiT, L_wet 0.05 m, h_film 3e4 W/m2K) and by saturation at T_intake; excess stays as bound-(ii) liquid. Fixed point on the EVO state.
- Module 06 budget formula check (CH3OH CR24/w0.6/T360, legacy): recovered 4.604 kW (Module 06: 4.61), upstream need 2.017 kW (Module 06: 2.02).
- flame_T fix: the 32b6350 debit used gas formation enthalpy h_ref with a kg/cycle liquid mass (mixed U/H bases, all water counted in Yc); restored to the per-kg-fuel H basis (1-e) m_w h_latent with the water vapour in the products.

NO_rel is NO per J of fuel at the wet point divided by NO per J of fuel at the dry point with the same CR, T_intake and regime. T_b is the peak burned-zone temperature. The ~1e-5 for methanol water-majority (legacy schedule 1.26e-5; coupled bound (ii) 1.0e-5 to 7.9e-5) is a temperature effect, not a bug. At w = 0.6 the water's latent heat and its heat capacity as a diluent lower T_b from about 2470-2520 K (dry) to about 1800-1910 K. Thermal NO is controlled by N2 + O -> NO + N (activation temperature 38370 K), and the O-atom concentration is itself in equilibrium with O2 (another ~31000 K). So the initial NO formation rate goes as T^-0.5 exp(-69090/T) [O2]^0.5 [N2] (Heywood 1988, eq. 11.8). On peak T_b alone that predicts 3.3e-5 for the methanol legacy row (computed 1.26e-5) and about 1-2e-4 for coupled bound (ii) (computed 1.0e-5 to 7.9e-5). The rest of the suppression (a factor of about 0.4 for legacy and 0.1-0.6 for bound (ii)) comes from lower O2/N2 mole fractions (dilution by water vapour), a shorter time near peak T, and in bound (ii) the liquid sink cooling the burned zone while it is still hot. The bare 38370 K exponent (about 3e-3 to 8e-3) misses the suppression by 2-3 orders, so it is not the right yardstick. Ethanol water-majority runs hotter (T_b 1980-2100 K), so NO_rel is about 3e-4 to 2e-3. Bound (i) evaporates the film upstream, which also leaves a hotter charge (higher T_b) and gives NO_rel 1e-4 to 2e-2. This is executed only: the NO is Module 03 Zeldovich in the burned zone, not cross-checked by M03-CRN and not validated against experiment.

Port-wall capacity data (wme1-hiT) is executed, not verified. Best dry is re-selected per regime but the wet points are the normal-regime design points (CR/w), not re-optimised.
